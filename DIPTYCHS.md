# SAME DIFFERENCE: how to frame a homophone diptych

Every pair is two New Yorkers who sound the same and live completely different lives. A plain left and right
split is the floor, not the ceiling. Below are fifteen frames that make the pair *move*, each with three variants,
the thing that drives it (what makes it dynamic), the pairs it suits best, and where it lives: the gallery
(SAME DIFFERENCE), a post on X, or a dynamic token.

The seven drivers, so you can mix them:

| Driver | What flips the picture |
|---|---|
| **Where you stand** | your position or angle (lenticular, anamorphic, peephole) |
| **What you do** | click, scratch, peel, fold, pick up a phone |
| **New York time** | the real clock, the real sunset, the real weather in the city |
| **Physics** | wind, a train going by, a door closing, a spin |
| **Light** | a switch, a flicker, a flashlight, a shadow crossing |
| **Sound** | a word said out loud arrives as the other word |
| **The chain** | a sale, a holder's pin, a vote, a mint count |

---

## 1. COMING // GOING (lenticular)
A wall of vertical prism slats, like a ridged subway ad. Walk one way and it's word A, walk back and it's word B.
- **a. The platform ad**: uptown riders see PROFIT, downtown riders see PROPHET.
- **b. The MetroCard**: a card on your phone that flips when you tilt it (gyroscope).
- **c. The bus shelter**: three angles, A, the seam where both show at once (the funniest frame), B.
- **Driver**: where you stand. **Best pairs**: Profit // Prophet, Knew // New, Miner // Minor, Idle // Idol, Peace // Piece, Brake // Break.
- **Lives**: gallery room 1. On X as a 6 s walk past GIF.

## 2. DEPARTURES (split flap)
The Grand Central board. The word flips letter by letter, MORNING clacks into MOURNING, and the picture below
flips in strips at the same time.
- **a. Letter by letter**: only the changed letters flip, so the eye catches the one letter that matters.
- **b. The picture board**: the painting is cut into flaps and flips top to bottom.
- **c. On the hour**: it flips on the real New York hour, like the station clock.
- **Driver**: New York time, what you do. **Best pairs**: Morning // Mourning, Plain // Plane, Flew // Flu, Road // Rode, Hour // Our, Sea // See.
- **Lives**: gallery room 2. A dynamic token that flips on the hour.

## 3. OPEN // CLOSED (the two sided sign)
The sign hanging in a bodega door: one side A, one side B, spinning on its string.
- **a. Walk by and it spins**: your draft turns it.
- **b. The deli menu board**: every item is a hanging two sided card, so the whole wall shimmers as you pass.
- **c. The barber pole**: both pictures wrapped as a spiral, A climbing, B falling, forever.
- **Driver**: physics. **Best pairs**: Or // Oar, Role // Roll, Bread // Bred, Flour // Flower, Meat // Meet, Leak // Leek.
- **Lives**: gallery room 3 (the deli). On X as a spinning sign GIF.

## 4. THE TWO WAY MIRROR
An interrogation room. Light on your side: the glass is a mirror and shows A. Light on their side: you see
through to B.
- **a. The light switch**: click it, the room goes dark, the other side appears.
- **b. Pepper's ghost**: B floats in the glass over A, half there.
- **c. The flashlight**: on a phone, your torch is the only light, and only what it touches flips.
- **Driver**: light. **Best pairs**: Liar // Lyre, Cell // Sell, Council // Counsel, Principal // Principle, Heard // Herd, Gilt // Guilt.
- **Lives**: gallery room 4. On X as a lights on, lights off two frame post.

## 5. THE WHISPERING GALLERY
Under Grand Central there is an arch where you whisper in one corner and someone hears you in the opposite
one. Here the word goes in as one homophone and arrives as the other.
- **a. The arch**: say TAWK into the corner, TOCK lights up across the way.
- **b. Two payphones**: pick one up, the other rings, and it's the other word.
- **c. The tin can phone**: across an airshaft between two windows.
- **Driver**: sound. **Best pairs**: the New York accent set, Tawk // Tock, Sawce // Sawce, Cah // Caw, Toid // Toid.
- **Lives**: gallery room 5. On X as a video with sound, the only frame that needs the accent heard.

## 6. MANHATTANHENGE (day // night)
The token or the wall shows A in daylight and B after the real sunset in New York.
- **a. The sunset swap**: computed from the real NYC sunset, so it flips at a different minute every day.
- **b. The shadow sweep**: a building's shadow crosses the pair and flips it strip by strip.
- **c. The weather**: Rain // Reign shows the crown when the sun is out and the umbrella seller when it rains in the city.
- **Driver**: New York time. **Best pairs**: Son // Sun, Knight // Night, Rain // Reign, Morning // Mourning, Sea // See.
- **Lives**: gallery room 6. The strongest dynamic token in the set, runs with no contract (like THE EVICTION's year).

## 7. THE TUNNEL BLINK
The subway car's lights flicker in the tunnel and every ad card above the windows is the other word when the
lights come back.
- **a. The car card row**: eight cards, all flip on the same blink.
- **b. The window**: your reflection in the dark window is the other word.
- **c. Express // Local**: the express skips the flip, so you only see A.
- **Driver**: light, physics. **Best pairs**: Pole // Poll, Lone // Loan, Feat // Feet, Patience // Patients, Cereal // Serial, Fair // Fare, Cent // Scent, Flew // Flu.
- **Lives**: gallery room 7.

## 8. STAND CLEAR OF THE CLOSING DOORS
Each door leaf carries one word. The doors close and the two halves meet as one picture.
- **a. The ding dong**: the chime plays, the doors close, A and B become a single scene.
- **b. Held doors**: someone's arm keeps them apart, so the pair never meets (Patience // Patients).
- **c. Elevator**: the same idea in a pre war lift with a brass gate.
- **Driver**: physics. **Best pairs**: Pair // Pear, Made // Maid, Mail // Male, In // Inn.
- **Lives**: the gallery's doorways between rooms. On X as a 4 s loop.

## 9. ONE // WON (scratch off)
A bodega scratch off ticket. Scratch the silver and the other word is under it.
- **a. Scratch to reveal**: drag to scratch, the coin sound included.
- **b. The losing ticket**: you scratch and it's the same word twice. SAME DIFFERENCE.
- **c. The book**: a whole book of tickets, one pair each.
- **Driver**: what you do. **Best pairs**: One // Won, Cent // Scent, Buy // By, Loan // Lone.
- **Lives**: a game at the gallery exit. A mint mechanic for a free drop.

## 10. THE WHEAT PASTE PEEL
Posters pasted over posters on a construction wall. Peel the top one and the pair is underneath.
- **a. Peel corner**: drag a corner, it curls back.
- **b. Rain damage**: the top layer washes off a little every day it rains in New York.
- **c. Buffed**: the city paints over it in grey and the next one goes up.
- **Driver**: what you do, New York time. **Best pairs**: Site // Cite, Base // Bass, Board // Bored, Ceiling // Sealing.
- **Lives**: the poster wall in the gallery.

## 11. THE FOLD IN
A picture that's word A flat, and becomes word B when you fold it inward along two lines.
- **a. Paper**: a real print with the fold marks printed on the back.
- **b. Animated**: the fold played as a GIF.
- **c. Tri fold**: three panels, the middle one hides when folded.
- **Driver**: what you do. **Best pairs**: Hole // Whole, Peak // Peek, Grate // Great.
- **Lives**: a print for the shop. On X as the fold GIF.

## 12. THE ACCENT TOGGLE
One picture, one button: say it like a tourist, say it like a New Yorker. The word and the picture change.
- **a. Speaker button**: the voice says COFFEE, then CAWFEE.
- **b. The subtitle**: the captions are spelled phonetically.
- **c. The borough dial**: Manhattan, Brooklyn, Bronx, Queens, Staten Island, each a little further from the dictionary.
- **Driver**: sound. **Best pairs**: the accent set.
- **Lives**: the Whispering Gallery and the MLOWTAR booth.

## 13. PICK A SIDE (the chain)
The holder decides which half shows. Or the city votes.
- **a. The pin**: like THE EVICTION's year, the holder pins A or B.
- **b. The vote**: every wallet picks a side; the majority shows.
- **c. Buy // By**: the piece shows BUY until it sells, then BY, with the collector's name.
- **Driver**: the chain. **Best pairs**: Buy // By, Pole // Poll, One // Won, Earn // Urn.
- **Lives**: a dynamic token. Runs as a pin with no contract first.

## 14. THE MORPH LOOP
A 6 s loop that turns A into B and back. Dreamina first and last frame: A then B, then B then A.
- **a. Clean dissolve**: the eye flower head stays in place while everything around it changes.
- **b. Walk through**: the person walks behind a pole and comes out as the other one.
- **c. The quick change**: a spin, and they're the other word.
- **Driver**: time. **Best pairs**: Flower // Flour, Heal // Heel, Hair // Hare, Bare // Bear.
- **Lives**: X and the token animation.

## 15. THE CHALK ON THE FLOOR (anamorphic)
Two anamorphic paintings on one floor. From the door it reads A. From the far end it reads B.
- **a. The sidewalk**: chalk at the gallery entrance.
- **b. The stairs**: one word on the risers going up, the other going down.
- **c. The ceiling**: look up at the right spot.
- **Driver**: where you stand. **Best pairs**: Sea // See, Hear // Here, Right // Write.
- **Lives**: the gallery, between rooms.

## How a post on X reads (the caption rule)
Name the thing, one reason to care, what to do. For example: **PROFIT // PROPHET**, two New Yorkers who both
call the crash, only one gets a bonus. Walk past it in SAME DIFFERENCE at n3wyorkers.com/same.
