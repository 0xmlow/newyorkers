# Claude's draft, before Astra (merge later)

Thesis: you drop through a manhole on First Avenue and land at rat scale. Every human thing down here is colossal
(the CC0 props at 10x to 40x). The rats run a parallel city out of what New York drops, flushes and forgets.

1. ARRIVALS (the manhole): a customs hall under the street. Trick: the fall, shrink to rat scale.
2. THE SLICE (First Ave L, Sept 2015): a shrine to Pizza Rat on the station stairs. Trick: drag the slice up the stairs.
3. FLUSHED (the goldfish aquarium): every goldfish ever flushed. Trick: the water rises to your eye line.
4. THE GATOR ROOM (East 123rd St, Feb 1935): the alligator lounge. Trick: stand still and eyes surface.
5. THE FATBERG (a cathedral of grease). Trick: the walls drip toward you.
6. THE CRUMB EXCHANGE (rat race). Trick: live board, buy crumbs low.
7. LOST THROUGH THE GRATE: AirPods, MetroCards, keys, rings at boulder scale. Trick: things fall from the grate above.
8. BEACH PNEUMATIC (1870): the secret air subway. Trick: the fan blows you down the tube.
9. STEAM BATHS (Con Ed): the rat spa. Trick: steam fog that only clears where you walk.
10. CITY HALL LOOP (1904 station): the rat council. Trick: the loop.
11. THE BORDER (bodega cat checkpoint). Trick: the cat sees you only when you move.
12. THE THRONE / UP AND OUT: the Rat King. Trick: climb out the manhole back to the street at human size.
