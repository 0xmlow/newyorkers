lichtenstein_c: his Interiors (1990s paintings of rooms with art on the walls) and the 1993 Guggenheim retrospective. The hall is a Lichtenstein Interior you walk inside: every surface drawn in black outline, flat primary colour and Ben-Day dots, a sofa, a lamp, a plant, all flat, and MLow's pieces hang in it as the only fully detailed things. Trick: toon outline plus halftone shading on the architecture only, so the art pops; walk to the exact spot and the whole hall lines up into one framed comic panel.
dali_c: the Julien Levy Gallery, Madison Avenue, 1933, where The Persistence of Memory was first shown in America (1932 Surrealism show?) or MoMA's 1941 Dali retrospective. Trick: the frames and the pieces melt and drip down the wall while you stare at them, and set back when you look away; the floor is a long sloping perspective that is shorter than it looks.
warhol_c: Leo Castelli, April 1966, Cow Wallpaper and Silver Clouds, the show where he said he was retiring from painting. Pieces hang on cow wallpaper (we can use our own repeating eye flower cow), silver pillows float and drift when you walk into them. Trick: every piece hangs as a silkscreen grid that repeats it four or nine times in shifting inks as you walk past; the clouds bump and reflect the art.
