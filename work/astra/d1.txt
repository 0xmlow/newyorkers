kusama_c: the Gertrude Stein Gallery, 1963, Aggregation: One Thousand Boats Show (a rowboat covered in white stuffed soft forms in a room papered with 999 photos of the boat), merged with her mirrored rooms. The hall is mirrored; the pieces repeat forever in every direction and the soft boat sits in the middle. Trick: an infinity mirror hall, built with real reflectors or mirrored copies, so 28 pieces become thousands.
basquiat_c: the Annina Nosei Gallery basement, Prince Street, 1981 to 1982, where he painted under the gallery; canvases leaning, stretchers, paint, a mattress, a television. Or the 1981 New York/New Wave show at PS1 that put him in front of everyone. Trick: the basement ceiling is the gallery floor above, where visitors walk and look down through a skylight at the pieces being made; or the labels write themselves in his crossed out hand as you approach.
haring_c: the Pop Shop, 292 Lafayette Street, opened April 1986, every surface covered floor to ceiling in his black line mural, the shop selling art people could afford. Trick: the black lines on the walls are alive, they crawl from frame to frame and point you to the next piece; or a till where you can buy a piece for one dollar.
hokusai_c: a woodblock printshop and handscroll hall: Thirty Six Views of New York. Real hook: the Met has shown Hokusai prints; Japan Society on East 47th Street. Trick: a long scroll gallery that unrolls as you walk, and from one marked spot the Empire State Building sits in every piece like Fuji.
