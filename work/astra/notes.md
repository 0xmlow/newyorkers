## Brief corrections taken (2026-11-04)
- Banksy's Central Park stall did sell: three buyers, eight canvases, 420 dollars on October 12, 2013 (my draft said nobody bought).
- Kusama's 2013 infinity room line was on West 19th Street, not 22nd. Anatomic Explosion was 1968 at the Stock Exchange; the infinity box is a gallery addition, labelled as such.
- SAMO was Basquiat and Al Diaz together, 1977 to 1980; the crowns belong to the painting years, so room A keeps them to tags.
- Lichtenstein's comic paintings began in 1961; February 1962 was his first Castelli show (sold out before it opened).
- Accounts differ on how the Bonwit Teller tub went through the glass; the label does not claim a deliberate shove.
- The Unisphere is 1964, Dream of Venus 1939: the wrap is a fiction and says so. 4:20 clocks are a house rule, not Dali's.
- Silver Clouds debuted at Castelli in 1966; Empire was shot from the Time Life Building, not 34th Street.
- Picasso never visited the United States; Le Tricorne hung at the Four Seasons 1959 to 2014, then went to the New York Historical Society.
- The Empire State is not in a southwest view from DUMBO; kept as a painterly cypress, the room is a wrap.
