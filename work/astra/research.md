# Real exhibits borrowed for the third pass (research 2026-11-04, web, sources below)

Kusama: Obliteration Room, Queensland Art Gallery, APT4, 2002 (qagoma.qld.gov.au/stories/yayoi-kusama-the-obliteration-room). Narcissus Garden, Venice 1966, about 1,500 mirror balls sold for 1,200 lire each; restaged at Fort Tilden, Rockaway, 2018 (timeout.com/newyork/art/rockaway-2018-narcissus-garden-by-yayoi-kusama). I Who Have Arrived in Heaven, Zwirner Chelsea, Nov 8 to Dec 21 2013, about 40 seconds inside, two hour lines. Hirshhorn Infinity Mirrors 2017, 20 seconds a room, 475,000 visitors. The research also reported her death on Aug 14, 2026 (UPI, Aug 27); not verified here.
Basquiat: King Pleasure, Starrett-Lehigh Building, Chelsea, opened Apr 9 2022: childhood home, Great Jones studio and the Palladium Michael Todd Room rebuilt, Nu-Nile and Untitled (1985) rehung (archdaily, artnews). Unknown Notebooks, Brooklyn Museum 2015. Four Hands, Fondation Louis Vuitton 2023. Luna Luna at The Shed, Nov 2024 to Mar 2025, his Ferris wheel.
Haring: Art Is for Everybody, The Broad 2023: black lit club room with his mixtapes on a reel, Pop Shop ephemera (thebroad.org, flaunt.com). Pop Shop opened Apr 19 1986; its ceiling is at the New-York Historical Society. Crack is Wack Oct 1986, arrested. Luna Luna carousel.
Hokusai: Sumida Hokusai Museum explains the multicolour print step by step and demonstrates printing. Colophon to One Hundred Views of Mount Fuji, at 75: by 110 every dot and line will seem alive. No Western show taking the Wave apart block by block was confirmed, so the press table is modelled on Sumida.
Lichtenstein: Times Square Mural unveiled Sept 5 2002 (mta.info). Mirrors 1969 to 72 (whitney.org). Glass Labyrinth, Luna Luna. Look Mickey 1961. Studio to the Whitney 2022. No show with a magnified dot display was confirmed: the loupe here is ours.
Dali: Mae West Room, Figueres, from 1974 with Oscar Tusquets, steps up to a lens (salvador-dali.org). Dali Lives, Dali Museum St Petersburg, 2019, AI host, selfie at the end (dezeen). Dreams of Dali VR 2016. Dalidom, Luna Luna.
Warhol: Exploding Plastic Inevitable, the Dom, 23 St Marks Place, April 1966 (dsps.lib.uiowa.edu). Silver Clouds, Castelli April 1966 with Billy Kluver. Time Capsules, sealed 1974 to 1987, usually counted at 610, some opened live (carnegiemuseums.org).
Van Gogh: Immersive Van Gogh, Lighthouse Immersive, Pier 36, opened June 10 2021, about 800,000 visitors that summer. Atelier des Lumieres 2019. Meet Vincent van Gogh (his words from the letters). 903 letters survive, 820 by him. Letters 155 (1880), 638 (July 1888), 902 (July 23 1890); wording follows common translations.
Picasso: Forty Years of His Art, MoMA 1939, Guernica last. Dora Maar's 28 photographs of Guernica's stages, May to June 1937 (museoreinasofia.es). Guernica flew JFK to Madrid Sept 1981.
Banksy: Better Out Than In hotline, 1-800-656-4271 (hyperallergic). Banksy versus Bristol Museum 2009, works hidden in the collection. Dismaland 2015, three pounds. Girl with Balloon shredded at Sotheby's London, October 2018, just after selling for over a million pounds.
