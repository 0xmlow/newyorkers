## The real plan
Brooklyn Bridge Park runs 1.3 miles along the East River, 85 acres, Pier 1 opened March 2010. Fulton Ferry Landing is on the site of Brooklyn's first ferry landing (1642), its railing inscribed with Whitman. The bridge: main span 1,595.5 ft, towers of limestone, granite and Rosendale cement 278.25 ft above mean high water, each tower a pair of Gothic Revival pointed arches the roadways pass through, four main cables of 5,282 wires, 400 diagonal stays, deck trusses 33 ft deep.

| What the room has | What is real |
|---|---|
| Two main cables | Four main cables (fixed) |
| Towers 14 long along the bridge, 6.4 across, arches facing the visitor broadside | Towers are wider across the bridge than along it, and the arches open along the bridge axis |
| Tower separation 60, tower height about 36 (ratio 1.7) | Span 1,595.5 ft to tower height about 278 ft (ratio about 5.7) |
| Suspenders, stays, one side of deck truss | Suspenders, 400 stays, trusses each side |
| Granite promenade, timber piles, corten screens | Granite steps from salvaged bridge stone at Pier 1 |
