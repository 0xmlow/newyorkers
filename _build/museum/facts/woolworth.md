## The real plan
233 Broadway between Park Place and Barclay Street, facing City Hall Park across Broadway to the east. Cass Gilbert, neo Gothic, opened 24 April 1913, 792 ft, tallest in the world to 1929/1930. Four storeys of limestone, then glazed limestone coloured terracotta; the pyramidal crown green (originally gilt). The lobby is a cruciform arcade: two perpendicular double height barrel vaulted passages crossing under a dome, Skyros marble walls, glass mosaic ceilings in blue, green and gold with red accents, twelve grotesque brackets, and Jennewein's Labor and Commerce murals above the mezzanine where it crosses the north and south wings.
