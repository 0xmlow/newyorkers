## The real plan
1109 Fifth Avenue, northeast corner of Fifth and East 92nd Street, facing west over Fifth to Central Park. Lot about 102 by 100 feet.
C. P. H. Gilbert, 1907 to 1908, Chateauesque with French Gothic detail, Indiana limestone, slate mansard roof with dormers on the fifth and sixth floors.
Six floors and a basement. The 1963 List Building was rebuilt by Kevin Roche in 1990 to 1993 as a seven storey annex in matching limestone from the same quarry.
