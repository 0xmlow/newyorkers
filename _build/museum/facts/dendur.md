## Real plan
Gallery 131 (the former Sackler Wing, Roche Dinkeloo, opened 1978). The temple keeps its original east facing orientation: the gateway stands in front of the temple, a reflecting pool (the Nile) in front, a sloping granite wall (the cliffs) behind, and a sloped, stippled glass wall on the north side toward the park. The temple is 13.0 m long, 6.6 m wide, 4.9 m high, of pinkish Nubian sandstone, 661 blocks, 640 tons. Its pronaos front has two columns with composite lotus capitals.
