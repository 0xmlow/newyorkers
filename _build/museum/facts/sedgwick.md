# sedgwick: 1520 Sedgwick Avenue, Morris Heights, the Bronx

## Sources
- 1520 Sedgwick Avenue, Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1520_Sedgwick_Avenue

## The real plan
An eighteen floor brick apartment building completed in 1967, 102 apartments, 119,919 square feet, on the Cross Bronx
Expressway in Morris Heights. The 1973 party was in the building's recreation room. Formerly Mitchell Lama.
