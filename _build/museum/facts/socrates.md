## The real plan
32-01 Vernon Boulevard at Broadway, Long Island City and Astoria, on the East River across from the northern end of Roosevelt Island, looking at Hell Gate. About four acres of open lawn on a former landfill and illegal dumpsite on Gibbs Point, over the buried mouth of Sunswick Creek. Created 1986 by Mark di Suvero, permanent city park 1998. Entrance on Vernon Boulevard with a billboard that is itself an artwork since 1999. One block from the Noguchi Museum.
