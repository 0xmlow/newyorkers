## The real plan
The plaza is raised above Columbus Avenue and opens east. Revson Fountain (Philip Johnson, 1964, 38 ft across; WET rebuild 2009 with 353 jets in three rings, 272 lights, water to 60 ft) sits at its centre. The Metropolitan Opera House (Wallace Harrison, 16 September 1966) closes the west side: a white travertine east front of five concrete arches 96 ft high, with Chagall's two 30 by 36 ft murals on the south (The Triumph of Music) and north (The Sources of Music) walls of the lobby, eleven Lobmeyr chandeliers in the lobby and 21 in the hall. The David H. Koch Theater (Johnson and Burgee, 1964, the New York State Theater) is on the south side, David Geffen Hall on the north.
