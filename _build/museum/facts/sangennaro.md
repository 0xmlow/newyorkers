## The real plan
Eleven days each September on Mulberry Street in Little Italy, the street closed to cars, arches of lights over the
roadway, food stands both curbs, tenements with fire escapes. The Grand Procession leaves after Mass at the Church of the
Most Precious Blood (113 Baxter Street) at 2 p.m. on the last Saturday, carrying the statue of the saint, a half figure
bust in mitre and robes, with ribbon streamers that donations are pinned to.
