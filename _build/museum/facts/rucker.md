## The real plan
155th Street and Frederick Douglass Boulevard, 3.05 acres, at the foot of Coogan's Bluff and just east of the former
Polo Grounds site, with the Polo Grounds Towers beside it and the Harlem River and the Macombs Dam Bridge to the east.
One full basketball court inside a chain link fence with bleachers, plus a baseball field, handball courts, a children's
playground, bathrooms and a spray shower.
