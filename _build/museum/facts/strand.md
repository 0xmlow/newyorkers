# strand: the Strand Bookstore, 826 to 828 Broadway at East 12th Street

## Sources
- Strand Bookstore, Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_Bookstore

## The real plan
An eleven storey Renaissance Revival store and loft building with a steel frame, designed by William H. Birkmire,
1902, on the corner of Broadway and East 12th Street. The Strand moved in from Fourth Avenue's Book Row in 1956 (some
sources say 1957). Red awning, discount carts outside on the sidewalk, a long ground floor of tables and tall stacks,
a rare book room upstairs. Slogan 18 miles of books; the stock is now over 23 miles. Landmarked in 2019.
