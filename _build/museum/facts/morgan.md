# morgan: the Morgan Library, the East Room

## Sources
- Morgan Library & Museum, Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morgan_Library_%26_Museum

## The real plan
225 Madison Avenue at East 36th Street. McKim's 1902 to 1906 Classical Revival library of dry laid Tennessee marble on a 117 by 50 ft lot with a 73.5 ft centre extension (a T plan). The entrance is on the 36th Street front: a Palladian arch (14 ft opening between two 9 ft openings), a groin vaulted portico on four Ionic columns, six Doric pilasters, 16th century Florentine bronze doors, and Edward Clark Potter's two lionesses flanking the steps. Inside: the Rotunda, the West Room (Morgan's study, red damask), the North Room, and the East Room, the largest, with triple tiered bookcases, hidden stairs behind swinging cases, a 15th century Italian fireplace on the east wall under the Triumph of Avarice tapestry, and Mowbray's eighteen lunettes and zodiac spandrels.
