## The real place
The Bowery runs about 1.6 km from Chatham Square north to East 4th Street and Cooper Square. Houston Street splits it into the lower and upper Bowery; the Bowery Mural wall stands at the Houston corner. The Third Avenue El ran above it from 1878 to 1955. Landmarks: Bowery Savings Bank (130 Bowery, 1893), Bowery Mission (227 to 229 Bowery since 1909), New Museum (235 Bowery, 2007, with the OMA expansion at 231 opened March 2026), CBGB (315 Bowery, 1973 to 2006). The Bowery Historic District, Chatham Square to Astor Place, joined the National Register in 2013.
