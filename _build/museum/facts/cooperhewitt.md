# cooperhewitt: Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, the Andrew Carnegie Mansion, 2 East 91st Street

## Sources
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Carnegie_Mansion

## The real plan
Babb, Cook and Willard, 1899 to 1902, Georgian Revival with Beaux Arts touches, brick and limestone, at least 64 rooms over three basements and four storeys, steel frame, five electric elevators. A 1.2 acre site: the house on the north half facing 91st Street, the garden on the southern half toward 90th Street (secondary entrance on 90th added in the 2014 renovation), Fifth Avenue on the west. Metal fence with stone posts round the grounds. Metal and glass conservatory (600 guests). Grand staircase of imported Scottish oak. The 3,000 pipe Aeolian-Skinner organ was removed in 1974. Smithsonian museum since 7 October 1976.
