## The real plan
Broadway and Seventh Avenue cross in a bow tie from 42nd to 47th. One Times Square (25 stories, 363 ft, 417 ft with spire) stands at the SOUTH end, the ball on its roof. The TKTS booth and red steps are at the NORTH end in Father Duffy Square (47th); the 27 red glass steps rise northward to 16 ft, so people sitting on them face SOUTH toward One Times Square. The Neuhaus sound grate is on the pedestrian island between 45th and 46th. Illuminated signs have been required by zoning since 1987.
