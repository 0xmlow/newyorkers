## The real plan
A 28 acre public garden (infobox says 20.9) in Hudson Hill, Riverdale, on the bluff over the Hudson with the Palisades across. Wave Hill House (1843, gray fieldstone, William Lewis Morris) with Armor Hall (Bashford Dean); Glyndor House (1927, galleries); Perkins Visitor Center in the old garage. Gardens: the Italianate Pergola framing the river and the Palisades, the Flower Garden in a rustic cedar fence (a rose garden until 1969), the Marco Polo Stufano Conservatory (Palm House, Tropical House, Cactus and Succulent House), Wild Garden, Aquatic and Monocot Gardens, T. H. Everett Alpine House, the 10 acre Abrons Woodland. A Great Tree American elm (1985).
