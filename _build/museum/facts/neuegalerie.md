## The real plan
The William Starr Miller House, Carrere and Hastings, 1914, Louis XIII style modelled on the Place des Vosges: red brick with limestone trim, a slate mansard with dormers and bullseye windows, six storeys. The narrow front faces Fifth Avenue and Central Park; the bulk of the house and the entrance face East 86th Street. Cafe Sabarsky on the ground floor (Hoffmann lighting, Loos furniture, 1912 Otto Wagner fabric), galleries above, Klimt's Adele Bloch-Bauer I upstairs. Converted by Annabelle Selldorf, opened 16 November 2001.

## FIXED
- Mansion walls in red brick; limestone kept on cornice, window surrounds and door arch.
- Slate mansard with five dormers on the avenue front.
- Park trees as American elm with some oak.
- Eggs: neuegalerie-vosges, neuegalerie-cottage, neuegalerie-yivo, neuegalerie-sabarsky, neuegalerie-adele.

## LEFT
- Orientation: the long side and the entrance belong on 86th Street, with the narrow front on Fifth. Turning the house would move the entrance, the stair, the cafe and every hung work.
- Bullseye windows in the mansard and the Wagner banquettes are not modelled.
- Pre-existing audit note: one mount is reported unreachable and rescued; untouched.
