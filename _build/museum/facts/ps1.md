## The real plan
22-01 Jackson Avenue, Long Island City. A public school building from 1892, opened as the P.S. 1 Contemporary Art Center in 1976 (founded 1971 by Alanna Heiss), renovated by Frederick Fisher and Partners and reopened October 1997, affiliated with MoMA in 2000, renamed MoMA PS1 in 2010. Concrete walled courtyard on Jackson Avenue (Warm Up). Long term works are built into the building: Turrell's Meeting (third floor), Saret's hole in the brick wall (third floor), Kentridge and Caivano in the stairs, Rist's floor video in the lobby, Artschwager's Exit bulbs and blps, Weiner on the doors, Orr's Wall Slits in the courtyard wall, LeWitt, Mullican and Melman in the boiler room. Free admission from January 2026 for three years.
