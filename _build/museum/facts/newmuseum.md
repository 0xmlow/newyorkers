## The real plan
SANAA (Kazuyo Sejima, Ryue Nishizawa) with Gensler, opened 1 December 2007, about $50 million, seven storeys of gallery boxes (ten storeys in all) rising about 175 feet. Seven boxes of different proportions stacked round a core and shifted off centre, skylights where they overhang, steel trusses so galleries are column free, two layers of aluminium mesh outside, glass at street level. Inside: poured concrete floors, white walls, exposed ceilings, bright green elevator cabs, a long narrow stair between the third and fourth floors. Next door, OMA's addition at 231 Bowery opened 21 March 2026.
