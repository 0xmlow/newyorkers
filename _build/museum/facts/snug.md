## The real plan
An 83 acre campus (once 130) on the Kill Van Kull, Staten Island's North Shore. Temple Row: five connected Greek Revival buildings, A to E, built 1831 to 1880, Building C (Minard Lafever, August 1833) in the centre with eight columns under its pediment. Landmarks since 14 October 1965 (with the chapel, six of the city's first 20). Music Hall 1892 (Robert W. Gibson). 1842 Greek Revival iron fence. Saint-Gaudens' Randall statue (replica since 1983). Behind: the New York Chinese Scholar's Garden, June 1999, built by 40 artisans from Suzhou: three ponds, a waterfall, two pavilions, moon gate.
