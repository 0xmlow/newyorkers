# brooklynbridge: the Brooklyn Bridge promenade

## Sources
- Brooklyn Bridge, Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn_Bridge

## The real plan
Suspension bridge opened 24 May 1883, main span 1,595.5 ft (486 m), total 6,016 ft. Two towers of limestone and Vinalhaven granite, 278 ft above mean high water, each pierced by a pair of Gothic pointed arches 117 ft tall and 33.75 ft wide for the roadways. Four main cables, 15.75 in across, 5,282 wires each; 1,088 to 1,520 suspenders and about 400 diagonal stays. Deck 85 ft wide, clearance 127 ft. The timber promenade runs down the middle, 18 ft above the roadways, 10 to 17 ft wide, with three lanes of traffic each side below.

## FIXED
- Promenade raised to 18 ft above the roadways (posts, slab under the tower and traffic follow).
- Arches resized to the real 117 by 33.75 ft and the open gap above them closed.
- Flagpoles and flags on both towers.
- Six eggs: brooklynbridge-wire, brooklynbridge-caisson, brooklynbridge-whiteflags, brooklynbridge-stampede, brooklynbridge-jumbo, brooklynbridge-vaults.

## LEFT
- Span is about 60 percent of the real 486 m: moving the Brooklyn tower means re-laying the deck, crowds, traffic wrap and the far skyline.
- Real tower is about 140 ft wide with a wide central pier and arches over the roadways; here the outer piers sit where the roadways run, and the four tower works hang on those piers. A real tower would strand them.
- Promenade is wider than the real 10 to 17 ft: narrowing it moves the lamp banner and rail panel works.
- The promenade splits around the central pier at each tower on the real bridge.
