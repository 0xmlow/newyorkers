# chrysler: The Chrysler Building, crown and lobby

## Sources
- Chrysler Building, Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chrysler_Building

## The real plan
East side of Lexington Avenue between 42nd and 43rd Streets. Main entrances on Lexington and on 42nd Street, a small one on 43rd. 77 floors, roof 925 ft, spire tip 1,046 ft. Setbacks at floors 16, 18, 23, 28 and 31. Radiator cap gargoyles, hubcap frieze and Mercury helmet ornaments at the 31st floor; eagles at the 61st floor. The crown is seven radiating terraced arches in Nirosta steel with triangular windows, lit by 580 fluorescent tubes since 1981. The lobby is triangular in plan, walls of African red stone (Wikipedia says granite), floor bands of yellow Siena travertine, light bars of Belgian blue marble and Mexican amber onyx, ceiling mural Transport and Human Endeavor (Edward Trumbull, 110 by 67 ft). 32 elevators in four banks, lotus pattern doors of steel and wood.

- Lobby floor tinted toward yellow Siena travertine.

## LEFT
- Triangular lobby plan: would move every wall and mount; the brief forbids moving mounts.
- 42nd Street entrance and the cross street: the room has one avenue only; adding a corner street would need new bounds.
- Setbacks at 16, 18, 23, 28: the base is one 62 high block; a true stepped base is a rebuild.
