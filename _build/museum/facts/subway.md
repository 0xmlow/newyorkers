# Below the city (room `subway`, src/rooms/a.ts)

The room depicts Broadway, Lafayette Street on the IND Sixth Avenue Line (signs read BROADWAY LAFAYETTE and B D F M, exit to Houston Street).

## Sources
- Broadway, Lafayette Street / Bleecker Street station, Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broadway%E2%80%93Lafayette_Street_station
- Subway FAQ: Facts and Figures, nycsubway.org: https://nycsubway.org/wiki/Subway_FAQ:_Facts_and_Figures

## The real place
Opened January 1, 1936. Express station, four tracks, two island platforms; B and M on one pair, D and F all times. Three centre staircases to a mezzanine, wide stairs at either side to three fare control areas, exits mainly on Houston Street. Opened with white tile and a blue band; now a blue trim line on a black border with small BROADWAY captions in white on black. Blue I beam columns, alternating ones carrying black name plates with white lettering. Artwork: Signal by Mel Chin (1998) with Peter Jemison. Free transfer to the southbound 6 at Bleecker from 1957, uptown transfer from September 25, 2012.
