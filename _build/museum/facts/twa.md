# twa: TWA Flight Center, departures 1962

## Sources

## The real plan
Eero Saarinen's terminal at JFK, used from 19 March 1962, dedicated 28 May 1962. A thin concrete shell in four parts on four Y shaped piers, 6,000 short tons, about 1.5 acres; headhouse 152 by 230 ft at ground level. Two red carpeted flight tubes, about 232 and 272 ft long. A Solari di Udine split flap board. Almost 58 million ceramic tiles. Closed 2001; landmark (headhouse interior and exterior) 1994; TWA Hotel since 15 May 2019 with 512 rooms, a rooftop infinity pool, and Connie, a Lockheed L-1649 Starliner cocktail lounge, on the apron.

## What the room has vs what is real
| Feature | Room | Real |
|---|---|---|
| Shell | two wing shells and a spine dome over a round glass drum | four shells on four Y piers over a long headhouse |
| Flight tubes | two, red floored | two, red carpeted: matches |
| Sunken lounge | red pit with curved seating | the Sunken Lounge, red: matches |
| Split flap board | dark board with lettered rows | Solari board: matches |
| Airliner | swept wing twin jet, single fin | Connie, Lockheed L-1649 Starliner: four props, straight wing, triple tail |

## FIXED
- The airliner is now a Starliner: straighter wing, four nacelles, tailplane with three red fins.
- Six eggs: twa-grapefruit, twa-opening, twa-solari, twa-tubes, twa-connie, twa-landmark.

## LEFT
- The shell is the wrong form: four shells on Y piers over a rectangular headhouse is a rebuild of the room and its 22 mounts.
- Propellers not modelled on the nacelles.
- Interior colours beyond the red were not sourced.
