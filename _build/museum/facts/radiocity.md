# radiocity: Radio City Music Hall

## Sources

## The real plan
Sixth Avenue at 50th Street, opened 27 December 1932, designed by Edward Durell Stone and Donald Deskey. The marquee wraps the corner of Sixth Avenue and 50th Street; three 90 ft vertical signs carry the name. The Grand Foyer is 165 ft long with Ezra Winter's 2,400 sq ft mural and two long tubular Caldwell chandeliers. The auditorium (5,960 seats originally, about 3,500 on the orchestra, three mezzanines, four aisles) is roofed by eight telescoping plaster bands overlapping two feet at 30 ft intervals, closing on a proscenium over 60 ft high and 100 ft wide meant as a setting sun. The Great Stage is 144 by 66.5 ft with a 50 ft turntable and three 70 ft lift sections; the curtain is 112 ft wide by 78 ft tall; the Wurlitzer plays from twin consoles either side of the stage.
