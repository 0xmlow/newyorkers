## Real plan
About half an acre, rectangular, two levels, approached from the west, with the western upper platform once a dining terrace under eight hornbeams. The lower terrace is sunk about two feet, paved in marble (Vermont in 1953, lighter Georgia marble since 2004), cut by two rectangular water channels spanned by marble platforms. The 1953 release excerpt: a 168 x 110 ft area, pools of 96 ft and 50 ft. North edge on West 54th Street: an 18 ft grey brick wall with ivy until 2004, now a 14 ft aluminium screen. Trees: birches, weeping beeches (since 1964).
