# stonewall: the Stonewall Inn and Christopher Park, Sheridan Square

## The real plan
53 Christopher Street (the bar) and 51 (now the monument visitor centre): two 1840s stables joined and refaced in 1930,
two storeys, brick at street level and stucco above, a wide storefront window flanked by arched entrances, three windows
on the second floor, a neon name sign in the window. Across the street, Christopher Park: a 0.19 acre (770 m2) triangle,
fenced since at least the late 1800s, with George Segal's Gay Liberation (two standing men, two seated women, 1992) and
a 1936 bronze of General Philip Sheridan.
