# panorama: the Panorama of the City of New York, Queens Museum

## Sources
- Panorama of the City of New York, Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panorama_of_the_City_of_New_York

## The real plan
A 9,335 sq ft model of all five boroughs at one inch to 100 ft (1:1200), built for the 1964 World's Fair by Lester Associates for Robert Moses, in 273 sections of 4 by 10 ft. About 895,000 structures after the 1992 update (835,000 originally); Far Rockaway was never installed. Model planes take off and land at LaGuardia on wires. The Twin Towers are still on it. An elevated walkway with ramps circles the model, partly cantilevered, with a glass floor section. In 1964 visitors rode simulated helicopter cars around it for 10 cents.
