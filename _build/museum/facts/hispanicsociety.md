## The real plan
Audubon Terrace, Broadway at 155th Street, on part of Audubon's estate Minniesland. Master plan and first buildings by Charles P. Huntington; main building 1907, library 1930. Indiana limestone neoclassical fronts round a sunken courtyard.
In the courtyard: Anna Hyatt Huntington's bronze El Cid (1927) with four bronze warriors at the pedestal corners, two flagpoles with bronze bases (Spain and the United States) joined by a reflecting pool, deer on the stair balusters, animal groups and the Don Quixote and Boabdil reliefs on the library.
Main court 98 by 40 ft, 35 ft high (30 x 12 x 11 m), a Spanish patio with an upper gallery on terracotta pillars. Sorolla Room: 14 paintings of 1911, over 200 linear feet.
