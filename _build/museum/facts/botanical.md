## The real plan
A 512 foot C of eleven glass pavilions arranged symmetrically around the Palm House, whose dome rises 90 feet. Designed by William R. Cobb of Lord & Burnham, built 1899 to 1902, inspired by the Palm House at Kew and the Crystal Palace. Steel, glass, cast iron and wood; most panes 16 inch, the Palm House 20 inch. Each pavilion is its own climate. An interior courtyard holds pools.
