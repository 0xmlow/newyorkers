## The real plan
175 Fifth Avenue. A triangular block bounded by Fifth Avenue (west), Broadway (east, the diagonal) and East 22nd Street (south); the narrow prow points north at East 23rd Street and Madison Square Park. The walls meet at about 25 degrees, the vertex about 6 ft wide. Three part facade like a column: three storey limestone base, glazed terracotta above; 18 bays on Fifth and on Broadway, 8 on 22nd Street. 20 storeys, 285 ft at opening in 1902; 22 storeys, 307 ft after the 1905 penthouse. The one storey cowcatcher shop wrapped the prow. North of it: the 1909 Hecla sidewalk clock at 200 Fifth Avenue and the 51 ft Worth obelisk at 25th Street.
