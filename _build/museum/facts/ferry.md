## The real plan
5.2 miles, about 25 minutes, Whitehall Terminal to St. George, 24 hours a day, free since July 1997. Current boats: Molinari class (Guy V. Molinari, Sen. John J. Marchi, Spirit of America, 310 ft, 4,427 passengers, 30 vehicles), Barberi class (310 ft, 6,000 passengers), Austen class (207 ft, 1,280), new Ollis class (310 ft). Orange boats; originally white, maroon from 1926, then orange (the reason, visibility in fog and snow, comes from a secondary source). Heading to Manhattan, Liberty is to the west, the port side, which the room has right.

| What the room has | What is real |
|---|---|
| Hull about 107 m overall | 310 ft (94.5 m) for the big classes |
| One central wheelhouse and funnel | Double ended boats; pilothouse arrangement not verified here |
| Liberty off the port side, skyline ahead | Consistent with a Manhattan bound crossing |
| Route board with an arrow, facing into the cabin | House rule bans arrows; it was inside the cabin wall |
| Two bow works, the wheel, a plinth and hat inside the cabin box | Hidden from view |

## FIXED
- Route board text 'MANHATTAN (double arrow) ST. GEORGE' to 'MANHATTAN  ·  ST. GEORGE' (no arrows) and turned to face the bow deck.
- The two bow mounts faced into the cabin box (audit: 2 unreachable, rescued). Turned to face the bow deck (rotation PI, on the cabin's outer face, targets on the bow deck). Audit after: 22 mounts, 0 unreachable, 0 stuck.
- Ship's wheel pulled out of the cabin wall to its outer face; plinth and captain's hat moved from inside the cabin to the starboard bow deck; bell moved from x 4 to x 7 so its post does not stand in front of a work.
- Six eggs: ferry-free-since-1997, ferry-25-minutes, ferry-liberty-side, ferry-vanderbilt, ferry-spirit-of-america, ferry-orange.
