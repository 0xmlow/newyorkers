## The real plan
A glass roofed court on the north side of the American Wing, its glass wall facing Central Park. The marble Second Branch Bank facade (Martin E. Thompson, 1822 to 1824) closes the south side as the entrance to the period rooms: two storeys, seven bays, a projecting centre with pediment, Ionic capitals. The four column Tiffany loggia from Laurelton Hall, monumental sculpture, Hunt's two bronze lampposts, a mezzanine balcony. Saint-Gaudens's gilded half size Diana is in Gallery 700 (per the Met record summary).

## FIXED
- Loggia cut to four columns, floral glass capitals added (coloured glass beads round each capital).
- Two Hunt lampposts either side of the bank front.
- Park trees now American elm with some oak, bare for winter.
- Eggs: metamerican-bank, metamerican-lamps, metamerican-loggia, metamerican-diana, metamerican-courtyard.
