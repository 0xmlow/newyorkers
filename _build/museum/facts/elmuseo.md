## The real plan
1230 Fifth Avenue at 104th Street, facing Central Park, in the Heckscher Foundation's 1922 building. Founded 1969 by Raphael Montanez Ortiz in a school classroom; here since 1977.
The 600 seat children's theatre (El Teatro) with 30 ft fairy tale murals and castle shaped chandeliers. The 2009 Gruzen Samton expansion roofed the old courtyard in glass as lobby, cafe and performance space.
Three Kings Day: honoured since 1977; the 2024 parade ran on Park Avenue from 106th to 115th Street with three live camels from Goshen, Connecticut.
