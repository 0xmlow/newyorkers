# domino: the Domino Sugar Refinery and Domino Park, Williamsburg

## Sources
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domino_Sugar_Refinery

## The real plan
The Pan, Filter and Finishing House (Theodore A. Havemeyer, Thomas Winslow, J. E. James), rebuilt after the fire of January 8, 1882 and finished by July 1883. 250 by 150 ft; pan and finishing houses 10 storeys and 130 ft, filter house 13 storeys and 155 ft with the chimney; brick walls 4 ft thick below, 2 ft above. 155 ft coal chimney, top enlarged in curved brick in the 1920s. Landmark 2007, closed 2004. Converted by PAU (Vishaan Chakrabarti) with a glass barrel vaulted structure inside the shell, reopened September 27, 2023. Domino Park (June 2018) on the East River with factory artifacts, gantry cranes and an elevated walkway; the Williamsburg Bridge to the south.

## FIXED
- Chimney cut to about 47 m with a wider curved brick crown.
- Vault raised: springs at 28 m, crown at 42 m, above the parapet; glass side walls with steel bands over a 6 m steel base; mezzanines up to 22.5 m.
- Six glass wall panels turned to face into the hall (6 backwards to 0).
- Six eggs: domino-chimney, domino-three-quarters, domino-fireproof, domino-walls, domino-vault, domino-water.

## LEFT
- Footprint and height of the shell (40 by 60 by 34 m against 76 by 46 by 40 m): changing them would move every panel and the census wall.
- The yellow Domino Sugar sign on the roof (the LED replica since 2022) is not modelled; the facade lettering stays.
- Syrup tanks and crane positions in the park were not checked against a plan.
