## The real plan
545 West 30th Street between 10th and 11th Avenues, beside the northern leg (spur) of the High Line. Diller Scofidio + Renfro with Rockwell Group, opened April 5, 2019.
An eight storey fixed base building (the Bloomberg Building) and a 120 ft tall telescoping shell, 4,000 ton steel frame clad in 43,000 sq ft of ETFE cushions, which rolls 140 ft on six bogie wheel assemblies (6 ft wheels) on rails to make the 17,000 sq ft McCourt (up to 3,000 seats).
Speed a quarter mile an hour, five minutes to roll; clearance to the base building about one and a half inches.

## FIXED
- Wheels: six bogies (three a side), each with a pair of 1.8 m wheels, instead of eight 3.6 m wheels.
- Shell speed slowed to the real quarter mile an hour; wheels now turn by distance travelled (the old tick spun them on the wrong formula).
- Added a simple High Line spur deck on steel columns beside the rails (west side), planted top, rails; columns in keepOut.
- Six eggs: theshed-bogies, -quarter-mile, -etfe, -inch-and-a-half, -mccourt, -bloomberg.

## LEFT
- Travel is still +/- 8 m, not 140 ft: a longer roll would carry the shell into the base building or off the hall floor where the mounts are. Needs the hall and mounts rebuilt round the real nested and deployed positions.
- The shell is a box with spheres for cushions; the real diagrid and the ETFE seams are not modelled.
- Wikipedia (eight wheels) and ENR (six bogies) disagree; I followed ENR.
- Pre existing audit: 2 unreachable mounts (rescued), not touched.
