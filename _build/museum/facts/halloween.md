## Real plan
Sixth Avenue from Spring Street to 16th Street, about 1.4 miles, on Halloween night from about 7 PM. About 50,000 costumed marchers (65,000 in 2024), 2 million spectators (1.5 million in 2024), more than 50 bands, large rod puppets carried by volunteers. The Jefferson Market Library clock tower stands on the southwest corner of Sixth Avenue and West 10th Street, High Victorian Gothic, 1874 to 1877.
