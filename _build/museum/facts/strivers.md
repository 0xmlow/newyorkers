## The real plan
Both sides of West 138th and West 139th Streets between Adam Clayton Powell Jr. Boulevard and Frederick Douglass Boulevard, 9.9 acres, built 1891 to 1893 by David H. King Jr. Three rows, three architects: James Brown Lord (red brick and brownstone, south side of 138th), Bruce Price and Clarence Luce (yellow brick, white limestone, terra cotta; north side of 138th and south side of 139th), Stanford White (dark brick, brownstone, terra cotta; north side of 139th). Houses about 18 to 22 ft wide and 50 ft deep, back to back around shared rear courtyards and alleys, with gate posts reading Private Road, Walk Your Horses.

## FIXED
- The row is now uniform yellow brick (Price and Luce, north side of West 138th) with two white limestone sill bands per house.
- Moved the six near side trees from inside the facades (x = 12.6) to the kerb (x = 8.8).
- Added the alley gate past the north end of the row: two piers, an iron gate, and the PRIVATE ROAD, WALK YOUR HORSES sign.
- Six eggs: strivers-horses, strivers-three-architects, strivers-landmark, strivers-empty, strivers-piano, strivers-plumbing.
