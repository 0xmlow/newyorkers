## The real plan
Built 1840/41 to 1851 under engineer William J. McAlpine, New York's first permanent dry dock and still in use; city landmark since September 23, 1975. Stepped walls faced with about 23,000 yards of Maine granite on a cement and flagstone foundation. A flat floor 30 ft wide over a great inverted stone arch. Designed for a vessel 320 ft long, 49.5 ft beam, 21 ft draft. A 1920s caisson at the mouth sinks when flooded to let the river in; emptying the 610,000 gallon basin takes over two hours. On Wallabout Bay.
