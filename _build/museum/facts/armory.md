## The real plan
643 Park Avenue, the whole block between Park and Lexington, 66th and 67th Streets. Charles W. Clinton, Gothic Revival, brick (chosen over granite to save money), built 1877 to 1880, opened September 30, 1880.
Head house on Park Avenue with three towers, the central one 100 ft. Behind it the drill hall: 200 by 300 ft, eleven wrought iron elliptical arches about 187 ft wide, trusses 75 ft high, roof peak 100 ft.
Veterans Room and library by Tiffany and Stanford White (1881); Herter Brothers rooms; twelve landmarked company rooms on the second floor; a 300 ft rifle range in the basement.
