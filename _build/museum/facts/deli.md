## The real type
An appetizing store sells the food that goes with bagels: smoked and pickled fish, fish spreads, cream cheese and other dairy, pickles, candy, nuts, dried fruit. Under kashrut it sells no meat (a kosher deli sells meat and no dairy). About 500 in the city in 1930 and 1950, fewer than ten by 2015.

## What the room has vs what is real
| Feature | Room | Real appetizing store |
|---|---|---|
| Hanging goods | salamis on a rail | no meat at all: smoked fish instead |
| Counter | long glass and marble cases | long refrigerated fish and cheese cases: matches |
| Take a number | ticket machine, NOW SERVING sign | common in the type: fits |
| Neon APPETIZING sign | yes | the word on the sign is right for the type |
| Tin ceiling, subway tile | yes | plausible for a pre war storefront; not tied to a source |
| SINCE 1914 | on the sign | Russ and Daughters' founding year; fine as homage |
