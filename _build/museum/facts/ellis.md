## Real plan
Main building by Boring and Tilton under James Knox Taylor, opened December 17, 1900, French Renaissance, steel frame, red brick in Flemish bond with limestone trim, four towers with copper clad cupolas on the corners of the central section, three great arches on the south facade. The registry room fills the central second floor: 200 x 100 ft, 56 ft ceiling, a Guastavino tile vault installed after the 1916 Black Tom explosion. A central staircase rose into it from the first floor until about 1911; benches replaced the iron railings. Historic ferry docks faced east, the slips sit on the basin that splits the island.
