## The real plan
On West 81st Street: a six storey glass cube by Polshek Partnership (James Polshek, Todd Schliemann), opened 19 February 2000 at $210 million. Inside, the 87 foot Hayden Sphere, apparently floating, carried by truss work; the Space Theater planetarium and the Big Bang Theater (36 foot screen over an 8 foot deep bowl). The Heilbrun Cosmic Pathway spirals round the sphere between the first and second floors. Scales of the Universe compares the sphere to other objects. Arthur Ross Terrace outside. Opened with an eight planet solar system display.
