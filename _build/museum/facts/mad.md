## The real plan
South side of Columbus Circle on an irregular 4,624 sq ft lot between Broadway and Eighth Avenue: sides 74 ft (the circle, curved), 76 ft (Broadway), 97 ft (58th Street), 41 ft (Eighth Avenue).
Edward Durell Stone's Gallery of Modern Art, 1964, white Vermont marble over a loggia of 27 concrete columns ("lollypops"). Reclad by Brad Cloepfil (Allied Works) 2005 to 2008 in 4 inch stippled white terracotta panels with deep grooves; ten floors above ground and two basements.
A band of windows near the top forms an H on the circle side and an I on the Eighth Avenue side (HI from the northwest). Restaurant Robert on the ninth floor (formerly the Gauguin Room).
