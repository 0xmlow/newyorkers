## The real plan
275 ft long, 120 ft wide, 125 ft high. Gold constellations on a turquoise ceiling, more than 2,500 stars, reversed left to right. Three round arched windows about 60 ft high on each of the east and west walls. Pink Tennessee marble floor. Balconies on the north, west and east sides; west staircase from 1913, east staircase added in the 1990s restoration. Ten globe chandeliers, 800 lb and 110 bulbs each. The information booth is topped by a four faced clock with 24 inch opalescent glass faces.

## LEFT
- Window orientation: the real arched windows are on the east and west walls, which are the short ends of the long axis. The room puts them along the long side walls. Moving them would move mounts and the light shafts, a rebuild.
- The second (east) staircase and the balconies.
- The real constellation figures and their exact positions; the room's stars are random.
- Whether the rocket hole sits near Pisces (one secondary source says so) is not verified, so the hole is placed generically.
- The whispering gallery is one level down outside the Oyster Bar, not in this room; its egg sits in a corner by the entrance.
