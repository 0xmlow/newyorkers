# The Vessel (room id: vessel)

## Sources

## The real plan
A 16 storey, 150 ft (46 m) honeycomb of connected staircases in the Hudson Yards Public Square, by Thomas Heatherwick: 154 flights, about 2,500 steps, 80 landings, copper coloured steel cladding, 50 ft wide at the base and 150 ft at the top. 75 pieces made in Monfalcone, Italy. Opened March 15, 2019; reopened October 2024 with mesh barriers and the top level closed.
