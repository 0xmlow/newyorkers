## The real plan
A two level hall, 19,000 square feet of floor, a balcony round the upper level with ocean ecosystem displays, 15 large dioramas on the lower level (walrus, sperm whale and giant squid, Andros coral reef and others). The 94 foot fiberglass and polyurethane blue whale (1969, about 21,000 pounds) hangs high in the centre with no wires, carried by an internal iron frame bolted to a single 16 inch steel pipe braced to the roof trusses, its back almost at the ceiling. Blue lighting.
