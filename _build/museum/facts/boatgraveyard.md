## The real plan
A working marine scrapyard on the Arthur Kill at Rossville, near the Fresh Kills landfill, founded in the 1930s by John J. Witte (Witte Marine Scrap Yard, the Tugboat Graveyard; officially the Donjon Iron and Metal Scrap Processing Facility since 2014). About 100 hulls remain from a high of about 400, mostly cargo ships and tugboats of the twentieth century, in mud and shallow water. Historic hulls included USS PC-1264 and the fireboat Abram S. Hewitt. New Jersey (Carteret) across the kill.
