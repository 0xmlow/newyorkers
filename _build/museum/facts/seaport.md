## The real plan
Schermerhorn Row: about 20 buildings on the block bounded by Fulton, Front, John and South Streets, the Fulton Street ones built 1811 to 1812, Federal style, four storeys (2 Fulton six, 12 Fulton five), plain red Flemish bond brick, originally under one shared slate roof; granite ground floors on Front and John. Built on landfill between Burling and Beekman Slips. The museum ship Wavertree (1885, wrought iron, full rigged) lies at the foot of Fulton Street, the Brooklyn Bridge to the north east.
