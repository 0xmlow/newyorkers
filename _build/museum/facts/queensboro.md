# Queensboro Plaza (room id: queensboro)

## Sources
- Queensboro Plaza station, Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queensboro_Plaza_station

## The real plan
An elevated station near the east end of the Queensboro Bridge. Two levels, four tracks, one island platform on each level; trains into Queens on the upper level, to Manhattan on the lower. On each level the 7 (Flushing) uses the south track and the N and W (Astoria) the north track, a cross platform transfer between A and B Division unique in the system. Exits through a mezzanine below the lower level. Built with eight tracks and four platforms (840 ft); the northern half closed in the late 1940s and was demolished in 1964.
