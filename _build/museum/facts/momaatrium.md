## The real plan
The lobby runs through the block from 53rd to 54th Street. Off it, Yoshio Taniguchi's 2004 atrium rises 110 feet through six storeys, white walls cut with openings from the galleries, a stair and bridges crossing at upper levels. The Abby Aldrich Rockefeller Sculpture Garden (1939, Philip Johnson redesign 1953) lies on the north side and is visible through big windows. The 54th Street side is black granite, dark grey glass and aluminium; the 1939 Goodwin and Stone building keeps its white marble facade. Expanded again in 2019 (Diller Scofidio + Renfro, Gensler).
