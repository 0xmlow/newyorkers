## The real plan
In Central Park's southeast corner, made from the western part of the Pond; opened 21 December 1950 on Kate Wollman's $600,000 gift. Closed 1980 when the floor buckled, rebuilt with brine in plastic pipes and reopened 1986. Victorian Gardens amusement park in summers 2003 to 2019; pickleball since 2023. The Midtown skyline along 59th Street rises to its south. Manhattan and Hartland schist outcrops around it.
