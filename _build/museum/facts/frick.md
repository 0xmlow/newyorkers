## The real plan
Thomas Hastings' house at 1 East 70th Street, completed 1914, Indiana limestone, behind a lawn on Fifth Avenue. John Russell Pope turned it into a museum (opened 1935): he roofed the old open carriage court in glass as the Garden Court, with paired Ionic columns, symmetrical planting beds, palms, and a long pool running the length of the court with a tiered marble fountain in the middle and two bronze frogs at the ends. Museum entrance on 70th Street through Hastings' relocated porte cochere. Russell Page garden on 70th Street (1977). Reopened April 17, 2025 after the Selldorf renovation.
