## The real plan
Wall Street runs east to west; Broad Street opens south from it (Nassau Street continues north). Federal Hall (26 Wall Street, Town and Davis, 1842, Tuckahoe marble, Doric portico, 18 granite steps, Ward's Washington statue on the steps) stands on the north side at the head of Broad, facing south. The New York Stock Exchange (18 Broad Street, George B. Post, 1903) is on the west side of Broad just south of Wall, its six fluted column screen and pediment facing east onto Broad Street; the 1922 annex at 11 Wall has 156 ft on Wall Street. 23 Wall Street, the House of Morgan (1914, four storeys, about 85 ft), is on the south east corner of Wall and Broad and still carries divots from the 1920 bombing.
