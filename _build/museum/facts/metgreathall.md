## The real plan
Fifth Avenue facade by Richard Morris Hunt, finished by his son Richard Howland Hunt, complete 1902. Four pairs of Corinthian columns, a sculpture niche between each pair, three arched openings, uncarved stone piles above each pair. Broad steps down to the David H. Koch Plaza (2014): two granite fountains, 106 London plane and littleleaf linden trees. Behind the facade the limestone Great Hall: three saucer domes on eight arches springing from big piers, flower arrangements in four pier niches and on the round central information desk. The Grand Staircase rises straight ahead (west) to European Paintings.

## FIXED
- Facade rebuilt as four column pairs around the existing niche mounts, a projecting entablature over each pair and a pile of uncarved blocks on top. Removed the floating piers.
- Information desk moved to the centre under the middle dome, flower arrangement on it; the middle urn removed.
- Koch Plaza allees: 8 London planes and 8 littleleaf lindens either side of the steps.
- Eggs: metgreathall-blocks, metgreathall-niches, metgreathall-plaza, metgreathall-flowers, metgreathall-accretion, metgreathall-hunts.

## LEFT
- The real hall has eight arches on big piers with the four flower niches cut into the piers; the room's columns stand in for piers. Reworking the hall's structure would move keepOuts near the hung works.
- The three arched entrances and caryatids are approximate; no figural sculpture by house rule on likeness and cost.
- The street runs perpendicular into the facade instead of along Fifth Avenue; moving it is a layout change.
