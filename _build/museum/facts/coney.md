## The real plan
The Riegelmann Boardwalk runs 2.7 miles along the beach, 80 ft wide for most of its length, planks in a modified chevron, 13 to 14 ft above sea level. Inland of it: the Wonder Wheel (1920, 150 ft, 24 cars: 8 white fixed, 16 sliding red and yellow or blue and yellow), the Cyclone (1927, 75 ft lift, 58.1 degree drop, brown wooden track, white steel frame, red fencing, red CYCLONE letters on the lift hill), and the Parachute Jump (250 ft, six sided red steel tower, twelve arms). Nathan's at Surf and Stillwell since 1916.

## FIXED
- Wonder Wheel cars: every third car (8) white and fixed to the rim, the other 16 red or blue with yellow roofs, sliding in and out along the spokes as the wheel turns.
- Parachute Jump raised from 34 to 46 units so it stands about 1.67 times the wheel, the real 250 to 150 ft ratio.
- Six easter eggs: coney-wonderwheel, coney-cyclone, coney-parachute, coney-riegelmann, coney-chevron, coney-nathans.
