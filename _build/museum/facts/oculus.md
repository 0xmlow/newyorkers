## The real plan
Santiago Calatrava, opened March 2016. A column free elliptical hall 350 ft long, 115 ft wide at its widest, 160 ft from floor to the highest point of the skylight. Paired white steel ribs (fireproof coated, stucco texture up close) rise from both sides to a skylight spine that runs the length of the hall; the spine panels retract. Floors and walls of Italian Lasa marble. Two levels of shop galleries line the sides. About 11,500 tons of steel. Aligned so the skylight throws the Wedge of Light across the floor each September 11 from 8:46 to 10:28.

## FIXED
- Hall lengthened (L 84 to 92, shell rows extended) so the end walls sit about 104 m apart, near the real 107 m.
- Section made taller than wide: crown 40 m, ends 26 m (was 24 and 15), end walls raised to 30 m. Real crown is 49 m.
- Six eggs: oculus-wedge-of-light, oculus-dove, oculus-wings-that-never-opened, oculus-steel, oculus-stucco-ribs, oculus-lasa-marble.
