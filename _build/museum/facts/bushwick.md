## The real place
Not one building: a generic loft roof in the Bushwick Collective's blocks around Troutman Street and St. Nicholas Avenue, where murals cover the industrial walls. Founded by Joseph Ficalora in 2012 from a mural jam. Town chartered as Boswijck in 1661; Brewer's Row (14 breweries in 14 blocks by 1890); the Broadway el (J, Z) on the neighbourhood's edge; the 1977 blackout looting along Broadway.
