# Top of the Rock (room id: rock)

## Sources

## The real plan
A 66 storey limestone slab, long axis east to west, with the observation levels on the 67th, 69th and 70th floors, about 850 ft up. 67 is a double height indoor observatory with escalators to 69; 69 has outdoor terraces behind an 8.5 ft frameless safety glass parapet (2005); 70 is an open roof, no glass, with a spherical beacon and celestial floor tiles. Central Park to the north, the Empire State Building to the south. Original 1933 deck 190 by 21 ft, decorated like an ocean liner.
