## The real plan
USS Intrepid (CV 11), an Essex class carrier, moored at Pier 86, West 46th Street and Twelfth Avenue, since June 1982. Angled flight deck to port, island to starboard. Space Shuttle Enterprise in the Space Shuttle Pavilion on the aft flight deck (on display July 19, 2012). Concorde G-BOAD on the pier (since 2003). Submarine Growler on the north side of Pier 86 (since 1988 to 1989). A-12 Blackbird among the aircraft. Pier 86 runs out from the shore into the Hudson, so the ship lies perpendicular to Twelfth Avenue, with New Jersey across the river.

## FIXED
- Turned the setting: river all round the bow and sides, the shore with Twelfth Avenue and two rows of West Side buildings off the stern, New Jersey skyline across the river to the west; Pier 86 now runs from the shore out along the ship.
- Concorde (white delta, drooped nose, four engine nacelles in two pods) on the pier, Growler (black hull, sail, missile hangar) beyond the north side of the pier.
- A brass plaque in the flight deck.
- Six eggs: intrepid-kamikaze, intrepid-enterprise, intrepid-stuck, intrepid-concorde, intrepid-growler, intrepid-stadium.
