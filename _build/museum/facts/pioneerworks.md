## The real plan
159 Pioneer Street, Red Hook, a few blocks from the water. Pioneer Iron Works, founded 1866 by Alexander Bass, burned 1881 and 1906 and rebuilt each time. Three storeys, heavy brick walls on fill with no real foundation, old growth pine beams up to about 40 ft long. A west facing brick wall of windows lights the Main Hall; a big round window at the top of the back wall. Third floor Science Studios. Garden on a former parking lot. Bought by Dustin Yellin in 2011, opened 2012, flooded by Sandy, reopened 2013. A rooftop observatory is planned for an 1895 telescope.
