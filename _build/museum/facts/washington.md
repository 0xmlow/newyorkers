## The real plan
A 9.75 acre park at the foot of Fifth Avenue. The marble arch (Stanford White, Tuckahoe marble, dedicated 1895, after a wooden arch of 1889) stands at the north edge where Fifth Avenue ends: 77 ft high, piers 30 ft apart, opening 47 ft high, about 57 ft wide overall. Spandrel victories by MacMonnies, keystone eagles by Martiny, two Washington statues on the north faces of the piers. The Tisch Fountain south of the arch, realigned with it in 2009. The Greek Revival Row of the 1830s on the north side. Chess players in the southwest corner. The Hangman's Elm, an English elm over 300 years old, in the northwest corner. Under it all, a former potter's field.
