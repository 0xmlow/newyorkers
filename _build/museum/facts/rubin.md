## The real plan
150 West 17th Street, Chelsea, just off Seventh Avenue. Barneys women's store of 1986 in six townhouses of about 1915 (138 to 154 West 17th), 70,000 square feet.
Andree Putman's steel and marble staircase spirals through the six storey centre, 108 steps. Beyer Blinder Belle remodelled it; museum opened October 2, 2004, closed the building October 6, 2024.
Entrance: the one storey Barneys door enlarged to a three storey mandala shaped gateway with a triple height void.
