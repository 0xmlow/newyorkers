# chelseahotel: the Hotel Chelsea, 222 West 23rd Street

## Sources
- Hotel Chelsea, Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hotel_Chelsea

## The real plan
Built 1883 to 1884 by Philip Hubert (Hubert, Pirsson and Co.), Queen Anne Revival and Victorian Gothic. Eleven storeys
on the 23rd Street front, twelve at the rear, about 180 ft (55 m) with a high mansard roof. Red brick with white stone
bands, flower ornamented iron balconies on floors two to eight by J.B. and J.M. Cornell. Brass plaques for residents by
the main entrance. Lobby with inlaid ceilings, mosaic floors and residents' paintings. A central staircase lit by a
roof skylight (sealed in the early 1950s). City landmark 1966, National Register 1977, reopened as a hotel 2022.
