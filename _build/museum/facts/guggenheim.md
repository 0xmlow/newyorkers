# guggenheim: the Guggenheim rotunda, Fifth Avenue

## The real plan
Frank Lloyd Wright, built 1956 to 1959, opened October 1959. A bowl shaped main gallery (six stories) wrapped by one helical ramp 1,416 ft long at a 5 percent grade in 70 sections, 25 ft wide at the bottom and 32 ft at the top, so the building widens as it rises (an inverted ziggurat). A solid 36 inch parapet on the atrium side. A dozen concrete ribs along the outer wall divide the ramp into bays; gallery walls tilt at 97 degrees under 9.5 ft ceilings. The domed skylight, about 95 ft up, is split into twelve sections by six hairpin spokes around a round glass centre. Ground floor of beige terrazzo inlaid with metal circles. Interior painted white.

## LEFT
- Turn count: the room has three turns, the real ramp about six stories. Changing TURNS moves every mount along the helix, which the brief forbids.
- The dozen radial wall ribs: at 30 degree spacing they would clip the 24 mounts set at 45 degree spacing per turn.
- 97 degree wall tilt: the outer wall ribbon and mounts are vertical; tilting needs the mounts tilted too.
- Outer shell widening (the inverted ziggurat seen from outside) is not modelled; the room is interior.
