## The real plan
One block, about 200 feet (61 m), with a sharp bend in the middle. It runs south from Pell Street, then southeast to the Bowery at Chatham Square and Division Street. It follows the line of an old stream. Four and five storey tenements both sides, ground floor shops, barbershops, Nom Wah at 13 Doyers just before the bend, the Chinatown post office at 6 Doyers near the Bowery end, the former Chinese theater at 5 to 7 Doyers (now a restaurant). Lanterns were strung over it in 2021, and the roadway carries the painted Rice Terraces mural (2021). A tunnel arcade runs from mid street to Chatham Square.
