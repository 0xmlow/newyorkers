## The real plan
281 Park Avenue South at 22nd Street, Gramercy. Six storeys, 1892 to 1894, Robert W. Gibson and Edward J. Neville Stent, Flemish Revival after the town halls of Haarlem and Amsterdam. Granite ground storey, Indiana limestone above, steel frame inside. Central porch entrance with an arch and a pediment with a bas relief, corner tourelles the full height, a second floor arcade of double width arches, round arched fifth floor openings, a mansard with dormers and a central gable with a cross and a statue of St. Paul. Fotografiska occupied it from December 2019 to 29 September 2024 (restaurant Veronika on the second floor, a bar in the former chapel).
