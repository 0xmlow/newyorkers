## Real plan
A double deck suspension bridge across the Narrows from Staten Island to Bay Ridge, Brooklyn (Brooklyn anchorage at Fort Lafayette by Fort Hamilton). Main span 4,260 ft (1,298 m), towers 693 ft (211 m), four main cables of 36 in, 228 ft clearance. Upper deck seven lanes, lower deck six. The marathon starts at Fort Wadsworth; runners use both sides of the upper level and the westbound lower level.
