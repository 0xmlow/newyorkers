## The real plan
On the Wednesday before Thanksgiving the balloons are inflated on West 77th and West 81st Streets between Central Park
West and Columbus Avenue, on both sides of the museum. The museum's Central Park West front is the Theodore Roosevelt
Memorial by John Russell Pope (dedicated 1936): pink granite, modelled on Roman arches, a 60 ft entrance arch, two
pairs of 48 ft columns with figures of Audubon, Boone, Lewis and Clark above them, on a 350 ft terrace. The equestrian
statue was removed in January 2022. The park side of Central Park West is lined with elms.

## FIXED
- Facade recoloured pink granite; pediment removed; entablature reoriented; parapet and four generic standing figures added; museum sign moved onto the entablature.
- Park trees set to elm.
- Six eggs: balloons-felix, balloons-reward, balloons-rubber, balloons-wind, balloons-explorers, balloons-statue.

## LEFT
- The 60 ft central entrance arch between the column pairs is not modelled; the census wall stands in front of where it would be.
- The real 77th Street side has the museum's older Romanesque south front, not modelled.
- Balloon count: real inflation holds around 16 or more giant balloons; the room has five.
