## The real plan
The Iris and B. Gerald Cantor Roof Garden opened August 1, 1987, on the fifth floor roof near the museum's southwestern corner, looking over the Central Park canopy to the Midtown skyline. One artist a summer since 1998, the Roof Garden Commission. Seasonal, with a café and bar; elevator lines on Friday and Saturday evenings. The Met's Tang Wing page gives it as about 7,500 square feet now, moving to a fourth floor terrace of nearly 10,000 square feet in the new wing (Frida Escobedo, due 2030). Wikipedia: closed for five years from October 2025.
