## The real plan
Studio 8H fills floors eight and nine of 30 Rockefeller Plaza. Built 1933 as the largest radio studio in the world,
132 by 78 feet (40 by 24 m) and three storeys tall; today it is listed at 6,102 square feet. Capacity cited as 1,200
(another source 1,400) in its radio days. The lobby: Champlain grey marble walls and desk, ivory marble piers, Sert's
American Progress (50 by 17 ft, 1937) at the centre and a 100 by 50 ft Sert ceiling mural (1941), Lee Lawrie's Wisdom
over the three entrance arches, five north south elevator banks.
