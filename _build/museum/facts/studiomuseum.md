## The real plan
144 West 125th Street. Founded 1968 in a rented loft at 2033 Fifth Avenue; on this site since 1982. New building opened November 15, 2025: seven storeys, five of exhibition space (82,000 sq ft), roof terrace, $160 million, glass and grey precast concrete, organised round an inverted staircase that references Harlem stoops. Designed by Adjaye Associates (the museum ended the relationship with David Adjaye by 2023).
