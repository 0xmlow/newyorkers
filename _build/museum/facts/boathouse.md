## The real plan
Built 1905 to 1907 by Helmle and Huberty on the northeast (eastern) shore of the Lullwater, facing west over the water for the sunset. Classical, after the first floor of Sansovino's Library of St. Mark, clad in white terracotta; an arcade toward the water and a canopy on Tuscan columns. Within 48 hours of demolition in September 1964; NRHP 1972; restored 1999; Audubon center since 2000. The Lullwater runs under the Lullwater and Terrace bridges into the 60 acre Prospect Lake, Brooklyn's only lake.
