## The real plan
A 10 acre park at the south tip of the island (West 8), opened July 2016, on fill added in the early 1900s. Four hills: Grassy Hill 26 ft, Slide Hill 36 ft (four slides, the longest 57 ft, the longest in the city), Discovery Hill 39 ft (Rachel Whiteread's Cabin, a concrete cast of a shed interior, with bronze casts of discarded objects around it), Outlook Hill 70 ft with the Scramble, granite blocks from the 1905 seawall. Views of the harbour, the Statue of Liberty, and the Manhattan, Brooklyn and Jersey City skylines. The plan called for 2,960 trees and 42,963 shrubs.
