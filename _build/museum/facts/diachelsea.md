## The real plan
Three contiguous buildings on the south side of West 22nd Street between Tenth and Eleventh Avenues: 535, 541 (the former Alcamo Marble Works, bought 2011) and 545 (bought 1992). Architecture Research Office joined them: 32,500 sq ft, about 20,000 sq ft of street level exhibition and programme space, single storey galleries lit by retained banks of skylights, three new brick facades meant to read as distinct but related buildings. Reopened April 2021. Along the block, 38 pairs of basalt stones and trees from Beuys's 7000 Oaks. Across the street, 548 West 22nd, Dia's first Chelsea building (1987 to 2004).
