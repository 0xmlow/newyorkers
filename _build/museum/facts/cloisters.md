## The real plan
The Cuxa Cloister sits at the centre of the museum (architect Charles Collens, opened May 1938). Its capitals and columns are original twelfth century pieces from Saint Michel de Cuxa (founded 878), cut from pink Languedoc marble; the walls are modern. The garth is a quadrangle split into four quadrants by crossed paths, an eight sided fountain at the crossing. Per the Met, each quadrant is a grass plot with a pollarded crabapple at its centre, edged with flowers and herbs. Red tile lean to roofs over the walks.
