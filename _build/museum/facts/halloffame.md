## The real plan
On the former NYU University Heights campus, now Bronx Community College. Stanford White's open air loggia, 630 ft long (500 ft plus a 130 ft annex), runs along the west side of the Hall of Languages, the Gould Memorial Library and the Hall of Philosophy, curving round the back of the library; it partly hides the retaining wall where the site drops west. Massachusetts granite parapets, Indiana limestone columns and pedestals, Guastavino tile ceiling, red Spanish tile roof. Places for 102 bronze busts (96 present in 2017), Tiffany designed plaques, Samuel Yellin iron gates at north and south with inscriptions. The library: copper tile dome, 16 green Connemara marble columns in the reading room, Tiffany glass.
