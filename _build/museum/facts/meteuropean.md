## The real plan
The Grand Staircase lands at the European Paintings suite, galleries 600 to 644, a run of top lit rooms in the 1880 to 1894 wings A, B and C. Each room is lit by a skylight over a glass ceiling (laylight) with interior louvers for sun control, replaced 2018 to 2023. Rooms open one into the next through aligned doorways, wood floors, benches in the middle, coloured walls.
