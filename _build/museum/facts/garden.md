## Real plan
A drum shaped building 153 ft high between Seventh and Eighth Avenues, 31st to 33rd Streets, on top of Penn Station. The roof is a 404 ft diameter cable suspension roof: 48 cables from a central hub (tension ring) to a rim (compression ring) carried on 48 steel columns. The arena floor is 45 ft above street level. Since 2013 two Chase Bridges, 233 ft by 22 ft, hang from the roof along the two long sides of the bowl, about 94 ft above the floor, on 24 and 26 steel tube hangers. Basketball capacity 19,812 (2013 on). Centre hung scoreboard over the court.
