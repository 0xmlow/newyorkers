## The real plan
32-37 Vernon Boulevard, Long Island City, on a triangular block parallel to the East River, one block from Socrates Sculpture Park. A 1920s red brick photoengraving plant (bought 1974 with a gas station, across from Noguchi's studio of 1961) plus a concrete block pavilion of the 1980s with Shoji Sadao. Area 1, the entry gallery, is open to the sky. Two floors, twelve galleries, about 24,000 sq ft, about 250 sculptures. Garden paved with granite railway stones, with katsura, Japanese black pines, cherries, bamboo, granite and travertine benches, and The Well (1982), a six sided basalt fountain. A 60 foot Tree of Heaven stood in the garden until March 2008.
