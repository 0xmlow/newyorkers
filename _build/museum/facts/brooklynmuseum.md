## The real plan
200 Eastern Parkway. McKim, Mead and White, west wing begun 1895, opened October 2, 1897; about one sixth of the planned building. Eastern Parkway front 510 feet wide, a central portico of six Ionic columns under a pediment with sculpture by Daniel Chester French, 30 surviving cornice figures (of 80 planned). Grand main steps removed in the 1930s; Polshek's glass entrance pavilion with four metal pylons and a semicircular plaza, April 2004. French's Brooklyn and Manhattan (1916, from the Manhattan Bridge) moved to the museum in 1963. 560,000 sq ft. Third floor Beaux-Arts Court: two storeys, colonnaded, skylit, a 10,000 sq ft central floor of terrazzo and glass block, the museum's largest public space.
