# tram: Roosevelt Island Tramway, Manhattan station

## Sources
- Roosevelt Island Tramway, Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roosevelt_Island_Tramway

## The real plan
Opened May 17, 1976; rebuilt by Poma in 2010 as a dual haul system (each cabin on its own track and haul cables). Two cabins; the originals were red, 12 by 24 ft, 125 passengers; the current ones carry 110. Line about 3,140 ft, 17 mph, three to four minutes. Three towers: between First and Second Avenues, at York Avenue (tallest, 250 ft), and on Roosevelt Island. Cabin floors at least 135 ft above the river. The Manhattan terminal is on the west side of Second Avenue between 59th and 60th Streets, six stories, with a deck built to carry a 32 or 35 story building. The line runs immediately north of and parallel to the Queensboro Bridge.
