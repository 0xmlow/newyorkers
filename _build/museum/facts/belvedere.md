## The real plan
Calvert Vaux and Jacob Wrey Mould, 1867 to 1869 (Conservancy gives 1872 for the whole complex). Manhattan schist dressed with gray granite on Vista Rock, a 130 foot outcrop, mid park at 79th Street. The built tower is the eastern one; the planned western tower was cut in 1870 and replaced by an open painted wood pavilion. Views north over Turtle Pond (2 acres, renamed 1987, on the old Croton receiving reservoir) and the Great Lawn, south over the Ramble. Delacorte Theater at the west end of the pond. Weather station since 1919: wind instruments on the tower, ASOS in a fenced area just south.

## FIXED
- Moved the tower to the east end (north east corner over the pond), with a block so it cannot be walked through. This also uncovers the west wall gilt mount that the old tower swallowed.
- Slate grey cone in place of the patina copper cap.
- Weather mast on the tower: flag, wind vane, cup anemometer.
- Small open wooden pavilion with a pyramid roof on the lower west terrace.
- Named species on the park trees.
