## The real plan
A basement under 178 Seventh Avenue South, the former Golden Triangle speakeasy, shaped like an isosceles triangle
("a piece of pie"). Fifteen steps down a narrow, steep stair. Officially 123 seats. Low ceiling, small round tables,
a bar along one side, a baby grand on a small stage.

## What the room has vs what is real
