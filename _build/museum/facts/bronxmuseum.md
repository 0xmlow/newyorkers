## The real plan
1040 Grand Concourse at 165th Street, west side of the boulevard. The south part is the 1961 synagogue of Young Israel of the Concourse, bought by the city in 1982; the north wing by Arquitectonica (2006, $19 million) has the pleated aluminum and glass front and doubled the museum to 33,000 sq ft. The Concourse here is 180 ft wide, briefly four roadways near 165th Street, lined by five and six storey apartment houses, many in the Grand Concourse Historic District (153rd to 167th, landmarked 2011).
