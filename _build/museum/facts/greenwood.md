# greenwood: Green-Wood Cemetery, Brooklyn

## Sources
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green-Wood_Cemetery

## The real plan
Incorporated 1838, 478 acres, about 570,000 interments. Rolling hills and kettle ponds left by the Harbor Hill Moraine. Main gate at Fifth Avenue and 25th Street: Richard Upjohn, Gothic Revival brownstone, 1861 to 1863, sculpture panels by John M. Moffitt, central clock tower 100 or 106 ft, monk parakeets nesting in the spires. Battle Hill (about 216 ft) is the highest point in Brooklyn, with Ruckstull's Altar to Liberty: Minerva (1919) facing the Statue of Liberty and the Civil War Soldiers' Monument.
