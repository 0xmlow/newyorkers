## The real plan
Two level terrace on the south shore of the Lake, at the north end of the Mall. The upper level flanks the 72nd Street Cross Drive; a central stair from the Mall descends into the Arcade under the drive, and the Arcade opens north onto the lower Esplanade. Two grand staircases flank the Arcade. The fountain (Angel of the Waters, Emma Stebbins, 1873) sits at the centre of the Esplanade in a large circular pool, the Lake and the Ramble beyond. Stone: mustard olive New Brunswick sandstone, granite steps, Roman brick paving laid on edge in herringbone. Arcade ceiling: nearly 16,000 Minton encaustic tiles in 49 panels on an iron (now stainless) suspension frame.
