## The real plan
A 12 acre state park at Hunters Point on the East River, opened May 1998, expanded 2009. Restored 1925 contained apron transfer bridges (James B. French patent) under black gantries lettered LONG ISLAND, which moved LIRR freight cars onto car floats. Four piers, one a fishing pier with a cleaning table. The Pepsi-Cola neon sign (1939, rebuilt 1993, 120 ft by 60 ft, ruby cursive) moved into the north end of the park in 2009, landmark 2016. Views west to the UN and Midtown.
