## The real plan
170 Central Park West, the west side of the avenue between 76th and 77th Streets, facing east onto the park. Central block by York and Sawyer, begun 1903, completed 1908; end wings by Walker and Gillette, 1937 to 1938. Gray granite, Roman Eclectic. A long rusticated base with rectangular windows, a ledge at the second floor, and on it eight three quarter engaged Ionic columns carrying the cornice, framed by plain, slightly projecting end pavilions. The 2011 renovation (Platt Byard Dovell White, $65 million) moved the main entrance to Central Park West and widened the stair. Library on the second floor, Gallery of Tiffany Lamps on the fourth. Douglass bronze at the 77th Street stair, Lincoln bronze on Central Park West.
