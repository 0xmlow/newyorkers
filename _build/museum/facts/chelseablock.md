## The real plan
West 24th Street between Tenth and Eleventh Avenues: low former garages and warehouses turned galleries (Gladstone at 515 in a garage, Gagosian at 555 since 1999, Matthew Marks also on the street), the High Line crossing the street mid block on a steel bridge, the Hudson at the west end beyond the West Side Highway. District runs roughly 16th to 28th Streets, more than 200 galleries.
