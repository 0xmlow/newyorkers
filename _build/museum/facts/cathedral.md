# St. John the Divine (room id: cathedral)

## Sources
- Cathedral of Saint John the Divine, Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathedral_of_Saint_John_the_Divine

## The real plan
1047 Amsterdam Avenue, main entrance on the axis of 112th Street, the west front facing the avenue. 601 ft long overall. Nave 248 ft long and 146 ft wide, vault 124 ft, eight sub-bays each side. Two unfinished west towers, the south about 50 ft taller than the north. Rose window 40 ft, 10,000 pieces of glass (Connick). Crossing under a 93 ft Guastavino tile dome (1909). Choir ringed by eight granite columns about 54 ft tall, ambulatory, and seven radiating Chapels of the Tongues. Great Organ, Skinner, 8,514 pipes; State Trumpet under the rose window.

## FIXED
- Swapped the west tower heights so the taller unfinished tower is on the south (+x) side.
- Rebuilt the east end: the choir wall now sits on the east half and above the ambulatory only, eight granite columns ring the choir, an ambulatory with an outer wall in line with the aisle walls and a stone ceiling, and the seven chapels moved out of the nave into their places beyond the ambulatory, each open toward the choir. The block that sealed the east end is gone; bounds extended to take the chapels; corner keep outs follow the round wall.
- The seven chapel works moved with their chapels and now face in; they were 4 unreachable before and are 0 now (audit: 23 mounts, 0 unreachable, 0 backwards, 0 stuck).
- Six eggs: cath-unfinished, cath-rose, cath-poets, cath-organ, cath-columns, cath-tongues.
