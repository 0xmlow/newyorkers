# rockaway: Rockaway Beach and Boardwalk

## Sources

## The real plan
Beach of about 7 miles from Beach 9th to Beach 149th Street; boardwalk 5.5 miles, Beach 9th to Beach 126th. Rebuilt in concrete after Sandy (2012), $274 million, finished May 2017: mostly 40 ft wide, bump outs of 5 to 15 ft at stairs, ramps and benches, sand retaining wall underneath, benches of reused boardwalk wood. Legal surfing at Beach 67th to 69th and 87th to 92nd. Ocean to the south, the A and the Rockaway Park Shuttle on elevated structures inland.
