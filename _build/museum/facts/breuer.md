# breuer: The Breuer Building, 945 Madison Avenue at 75th Street

## The real plan
Southeast corner of Madison and 75th. Marcel Breuer and Hamilton P. Smith, opened September 1966 as the Whitney's third home. Site about 104 by 125 feet, roof about 97 feet, five floors above ground. Concrete faced with 1,500 slabs of variegated grey granite; the upper floors step out over Madison (and 75th), the inverted ziggurat. Trapezoid windows angled 20 to 25 degrees from the sun: one oversized on Madison turned north, six on 75th tilted west. A sunken areaway (the moat) on Madison, crossed by a canopied concrete bridge. Lobby of bush hammered concrete with round ceiling lights; galleries with bluestone, teak or slate floors under a grid of concrete coffers; the fourth floor gallery is 17.5 feet high. Main stair at the south end. Met Breuer 2016 to 2020, Frick Madison 2021 to 2024, Sotheby's headquarters since 8 November 2025; city landmark 20 May 2025.
