## Real plan
Tudor City Place crosses 42nd Street on an overpass (1952, replacing a tunnel) between the brick Tudor Revival towers of Tudor City (Fred F. French, 1927 to 1930). Looking west, 42nd Street runs straight to the Hudson and the New Jersey horizon; the grid is rotated 29 degrees, so the sun sets down the street around May 28 and July 13. The Chrysler Building stands on the east side of Lexington between 42nd and 43rd, so from the overpass it rises on the right, to the north. Further west the Park Avenue viaduct wraps Grand Central over 42nd.
