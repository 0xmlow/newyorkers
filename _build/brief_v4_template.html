01

The short version

One token. Many lives. The collector picks which version of the city they live with, and the artist keeps painting new chapters into the same frame.

NEW YORKERS is a living census of the greatest city on earth, painted in psychedelic pop surrealism where flowers bloom with human iris eyes because the city is watching back. Since Brief V3 the collection has tripled: what was 344 subjects and ten families is now 1,842 painted characters across seven eras, a mythology with an origin seed under the subway, a 22 card tarot, a portrait of every hour of the day, and a mapped network of connections between pieces.

The census is numbered to 2266 and counting. Not every number is painted yet. That is the point.

04

The three marks

The Eye Flower

The city's attention. It blooms wherever a moment is truly witnessed. Its iris is human because the city's eyes are its people.

The Blue Evil Eye

Protection. It never moves, never blinks in stills. MLow's sovereign mark.

The Circle Grid Flower Sigil

Community. The Blossom mark. It appears where people have built something together.

Never swap the meanings

Attention, protection, community. Fixed since the Brand Batch, enforced in every asset.

05

Every character has a story now

All 1,842 characters carry a finished two sentence entry written to one voice law: first sentence ends with a period, second lands bare. Deadpan New York pride. No em dashes, no en dashes, anywhere, ever. The entries read like the best short posts on Humans of New York, which is the point: they are shareable one at a time.

"He runs the tunnels and collects tribute in dollar slices. A crooked crown, a third rail throne, an empire that never sleeps"

No. 0767, The Rat King. Every number can produce a post like this on demand.

06

States: what a collector gets

147 characters exist in two or more finished states, 2,701 finished files in total, plus 88 animation loops and 61 films. On Transient Labs ERC-7160 the states are the product: the artist appends, the collector pins. The census site demonstrates it with a compare slider on every multi state character.

07

The census experience

The collection ships with its own interactive 3D experience: every character rendered as one field that reforms into five shapes. The Iris (the City Bloom Map made literal), The Network (seven era clusters chained with visible connections), The Timeline, The Eras, The City. Plus an editorial gallery with caption plates, per character records, story entries, state sliders, inline loops and films, and a working curation mode.

08

Curation protocol

The final drop is curated, and the cut list is live data, not a memory. Curate mode in the gallery lets the artist cut or restore any piece; the export writes a curation file the build respects. One piece is currently cut: No. 0802, The Double Dutch Queens.

09

The drop

NFT NYC · September 2026

Dynamic tokens on Transient Labs ERC-7160, with OpenSea. Chapters are eras. The sets are quests: own your Hour, your Month, your Moon, your Line, complete the Tarot. The monuments are the grails. No. 1866, The Collector, is reserved: whoever holds it holds a portrait of themselves holding it. Charity partner: Chai Lifeline. All pricing surfaces remain illustrative, priced with partners against September conditions.

Rights framework unchanged from V3: public marketing leads with cleared subjects only; living public figures stay internal until counsel clears them; third party characters never appear on paid mint surfaces.

10

The story angle

The eye flowers in this art are the fLOWers. They came out of surviving cancer. The artist drew a city of eight million people while getting through the thing that nearly took him out of it.

MLow is Michael Low, a New York City born multidisciplinary digital artist. He trains custom AI models on his own photography, generates at volume, and hand finishes every piece in Photoshop. Public art on 5,000+ NYC taxi screens with Somo and Art Crush Gallery. Adobe Firefly Creative Partner Ambassador. Collected by Cozomo de' Medici, Jack Butcher, Matt Kane and Adam Weitsman. $250K+ raised for children's cancer causes through Art Basel Miami auctions. Chai Lifeline, the drop's charity partner, supported him through it. The throughline is Tripping and Rising.
