The drop tee, black, worn under the sash. Own pants, own shoes — enumerators look like New Yorkers, not brand ambassadors.
DO
Stay in character: polite, bureaucratic, slightly too formal. “Good morning. Have you been counted?”
Count anyone who wants to be counted, on the spot, free.
Hand every registrant their number verbally: “You are number one thousand two hundred four.”
Film everything; post nothing yourself — footage goes to the campaign account.
Move on the first sign of disinterest. The bit only works if it never insists.
DO NOT
Never claim to be the U.S. Census or any government agency. If asked: “It’s an artwork. A census of an art collection.”
Never mention price, mint, crypto, or NFTs on the street. The word is “counted.”
No enumeration inside subway cars or stations, no blocking sidewalks, no amplified sound.
Never photograph anyone without asking.
Never promise anything about value. Art, not an investment product.
