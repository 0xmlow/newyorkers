CENSUS BUREAU OF THE HIDDEN CITY · INTERNAL
The Guerrilla Playbook
Field operations for THE COUNT · Aug 24 – Sept 3, 2026 · every stunt feeds one number
THE MACHINE

Field Enumerator daily clips → live Count projection → borough leaderboard → The Posting on Sept 3. Every piece points at one number and one verb: GET COUNTED. Print props live in Guerrilla Kit; the projection screen is Count Projection (fullscreen it on any laptop + projector).

STANDING RULES: every printed piece carries "THIS IS AN ARTWORK, NOT A GOVERNMENT DOCUMENT." Never mimic the US Census Bureau seal or formatting. Stay with unattended objects, retrieve same day. Sidewalks stay passable. No real faces without consent. Publish the real count only — never inflated, never estimated.

01 · THE FIELD ENUMERATOR — THE FLAGSHIP, DAILY

Props: sash, clipboard + Form NY-6, COUNTED stamp, blank Resident Cards, ticket pad. Crew: agent + one shooter.

Script: "Excuse me — have you been counted? ... No? You're not in trouble. The city is taking a census of its characters and you're owed a number. Six questions, four minutes, it's free, and it's permanent." Run the six questions deadpan. Stamp. Hand the card: "Resident number [N]. Carried in the record forever. Welcome to the count."

Shot list: (1) title card, DAY __ OF THE COUNT · (2) the approach, wide · (3) close on the best answer (Q4 always delivers) · (4) the stamp, macro · (5) subject holding card to camera, says their number. 45–60s cut, vertical.

Caption: "Day 4. The Bureau counted a man who has lived above the same bodega for 31 years. Resident No. 1,287. GET COUNTED → link." Cadence: new neighborhood daily; pigeon episode (Field Report card) on the slowest news day.

⚠ Legal: talking to people on a sidewalk is legal. No megaphone without a sound permit. If someone declines camera, count them anyway, off camera.

02 · THE PROJECTION

Just the number on a blank wall, nightly, 20 minutes. No logo bigger than the URL. Shot list: (1) dark wall · (2) number snaps on · (3) it ticks up once while strangers watch · (4) passerby asks what it is; nobody answers. Caption: "The count doesn't sleep. 1,204." ⚠ Wall owner's written yes first — small businesses say yes free. Unpermitted projection is a nuisance summons.

03 · THE NOTICE

Door-hanger handed out and window-placed with permission — never poles, never subway (defacement fines per poster). Target blocks with the strongest characters. The disclaimer line is load-bearing: it is the difference between art and impersonation.

04 · NOW SERVING 8,300,000

Sandwich board + take-a-number dispenser outside the DMV on Atlantic, passport office, Apple Store on launch morning. Fill the COUNTED line with marker each hour, on camera. Tickets are pre-numbered from the live count. The clip: a person takes ticket A-317, agent says "the wait is currently forever. Or four minutes online." ⚠ Stay with it; keep 8 ft of sidewalk clear.

05 · THE 802 CUTS, SHREDDED

Livestream, one print per minute, silent, NOT COUNTED stamp on each before it feeds. Fixed camera, no cuts, no music until the last one. Announce only: "3,466 were made. 365 survive. Tonight the Bureau destroys the evidence." Save the shreds — they become Census Office set dressing and a future relic. Legal: your work, your shredder.

06 · THE REJECTION WALL

Permissioned wall, 20+ Rejection posters, red stamps. Pin a ballot box: "ONE COMES BACK. VOTE." Run the resurrection live at the Census Office on night two. ⚠ Wheatpaste only with the wall owner's yes; otherwise it's the fines-per-poster problem.

07 · THE HOURLY, LIVE

Launch day, 24 hours: post the current hour's character every hour, and photograph a person holding its print at the real location at the real hour. The endurance is the content — the 3 AM post should look like 3 AM. Story card in the Kit. Crew rotates; MLow does 11 PM – 4 AM (that's the arc).

08 · THE 59 LETTERS

Mail every community board its district's Resident Card + the letter (in the Kit). Costs 59 stamps. The win condition is one board reading it into public minutes — then screenshot the minutes and hand Gothamist the story. Follow up with borough press after the first bite.

09 · THE BOROUGH BEEF

Leaderboard card daily at 6 PM from real registration data. Copy rotates: "Staten Island: fewest counted since 1790." / "Queens passed Manhattan overnight. Manhattan has not commented." Never punch at people, only at boroughs.

10 · THE POSTING · SEPT 3

The finale. Broadsheet filled by hand with the real number, nailed to a board outside the Census Office, read aloud, two witnesses sign. One camera, one take, no music. Immediately announce the Claim date. Caption: "The count is closed. [N] New Yorkers, counted by name. 365 characters. The Claim opens in October." Post the making-of within 48 hours while the first wave peaks.
