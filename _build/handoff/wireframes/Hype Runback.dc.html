THE ONE LINE

“I’m a New Yorker.”

That is the thesis, condensed past the elevator pitch. Every post, space, and trailer earns its place by making someone else want to say it.

THE TRAILER ENGINE · SEEDANCE 2.0/2.5

The format: a quintessential New York moment, played straight — then the MLow world converges with the real one for a glimpse, never a tour. Cut to black. NEW YORKERS. 15 seconds, 16:9. Workflow per spot: build the 9-panel 16:9 sceneboard from the prompt below + one style-reference image from the archive, generate the board and prompt, drop both into Dreamina on Seedance fast. One trailer every second day, not daily.

SPOT 1 · THE LAUNDROMAT

15s, 16:9, cinematic 35mm, muted fluorescent color. A man sits alone in a Brooklyn laundromat at night watching a tumble dryer roll. Slow push-in on the drum — clothes tumble hypnotically. Cut to his face, half-lit. He glances out the storefront window: outside, the street has become a rolling ocean wave filled with blooming flowers [style ref: assets/hero/1467.jpg], neon reflecting off the water. Cut back — his eyes widen. Hard cut to black. White serif text: NEW YORKERS.

SPOT 2 · SHOWTIME

15s, 16:9, handheld documentary look. A tired rider on a rattling subway car. Doors open, two kids burst in — "IT'S SHOWTIME" — swing on the poles. The rider stares past them, annoyed, out the scratched window: on the passing platform, the tile mosaic is dissolving into the MLow flower world [style ref: assets/art/mz_mta.jpg], petals drifting through the station light. His annoyance fades to wonder. Cut to black. NEW YORKERS.

SPOT 3 · THE HOTDOG

15s, 16:9, golden-hour midtown. A woman buys a hotdog from a steam-fogged cart, hands over crumpled bills. As the vendor counts, she glances over her shoulder: down the block, the street is transforming — facades blooming into the MLow world [style ref: assets/art/mz_halal.jpg], an eye-flower opening over a walk signal. The vendor, unbothered: "Mustard?" Cut to black. NEW YORKERS.

SPOT 4 · CENTRAL PARK

15s, 16:9, summer haze, long lens. A man eats a soft-serve cone on a Central Park bench, heat shimmer over the meadow. A single petal lands on his knee. He looks up: across the lawn, the treeline is converging with the MLow world [style ref: assets/hero/2100.jpg] — flowers the size of kites over the skyline. The ice cream drips, forgotten, over his hand. Cut to black. NEW YORKERS.

SPOT 5 · I’M WALKING HERE

15s, 16:9, crosswalk chaos, wide anamorphic. A New Yorker steps off the curb; a yellow cab painted in the MLow livery [style ref: assets/art/st_cab.jpg] cuts the corner. He slams the hood — "HEY, I'M WALKIN' HERE!" The cab pulls away and drives straight into a cresting wave of flowers swallowing the avenue. He stands in the crosswalk, watching it go. Beat. Cut to black. NEW YORKERS. GET COUNTED.
