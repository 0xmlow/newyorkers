Fact sheet
NEW YORKERS · BY MLOW

NEW YORKERS by MLow is a living census of New York City: 2,266 characters and counting, from the Subway Samaritan to the Rat King to Midnight itself, painted in a psychedelic style where flowers bloom with human eyes because the city is watching back.

It has love stories that span 500 numbers, a mythology with an origin seed sleeping under the subway, a 22 card tarot, a portrait of every hour of the day, and a hidden blue evil eye protecting every frame.

It launches in two acts. THE COUNT: the Census Office opens at NFT.NYC, visitors get counted and matched with a character held in their name on a paper Resident Card. THE CLAIM: the chain opens and every hold becomes claimable as a dynamic token on ERC-7160, where the artist appends chapters and collectors pin the state they love.

The artist will sell 365 works. The other 3,101 are the published archive: evidence of curation, never inventory. The promise to never sell them is what makes the 365 worth owning.
