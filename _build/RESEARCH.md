# Research for CLOUDS AND DIRT V2 (2026-10-22), condensed. Verified unless marked unverified.

## Content and virality
- The content model: one pillar piece (keynote, vlog, Q and A, podcast) cut into micro content per platform; his 2018 claim: one keynote became 30 plus pieces and 35 million views. The free deck "How to Create 64 Pieces of Content in a Day", 270 pages, November 14, 2019. The $1.80 strategy: your two cents on ninety posts a day.
- Cadence: TikTok three to five times a day (Social Media Examiner, 2020), about 80 percent repurposed footage with overlays and text, 20 percent trends. AdAge 2024: "The official answer is as many [posts] as humanly possible." Team GaryVee TikTok: over 15 million subscribers (2025 hiring post).
- Verbatim lines: "I can't get over how underpriced attention on Instagram is." (2020). "I think the Super Bowl commercial is the most underpriced ad in the world." "Attention is the game. More specifically, underpriced attention is the opportunity." (Day Trading Attention). "By going narrow, you win." "The algorithm-driven nature of TikTok allows for organic reach that is unparalleled." (2024). "It's about capturing attention quickly and delivering value instantly." The hook is "the first few seconds of any video you make" and must be "a moment that exists somewhere in the clip you're making." "The title of the clip has to be punchy, and catch attention." Subtitles on every video because people scroll with the sound off. "The distribution now happens with algorithms, and that is a massive land grab" (2024). "the creative is the variable of success" (2019). "macro patience, micro speed." (2017).
- The visual formula: a bold title across the frame, key words capitalised, burned in subtitles, a progress bar, the logo, square or 4:5 crops, the hook taken from inside the clip. DRock (David Rock) the videographer, Tyler Babin, #JoinTeamGaryVee 2019 open sourced raw footage for outside editors.
- Most viral: a May 30, 2021 TikTok talking to a mother about college for her son, about 30 million plays and 4.2 million likes in four months; Charli D'Amelio tagging him, November 2019, about 30 million plays; "Monday Morning Motivation" over 10 million views on Facebook. YouTube about 695 million views, Shorts about 77 percent of them.
- Shows: AskGaryVee (2014), DailyVee (2015), WeeklyVee (2020), Trash Talk (2018, garage sale flipping: a $7 Skylander box listed at $182.49; $100 to $1,000 a weekend), Tea with GaryVee (2020, moved to Whatnot 2024), Overrated Underrated, the midnight Topps Chrome 2025 livestream on Whatnot. "You're gonna die" came from a fan asking for three words that could change her life.
- Unverified as verbatim: "TikTok is the most underpriced attention", "make the thumb stop", "attention is the asset" (closest: "attention is the single most important asset in business", SBC 2025).

## VeeFriends TCG (official rules, March 2026)

A ScoreSprint token once a game. Attacker announces Aura, Skill or Stamina; defender accepts or counters with another attribute, up to Total Score; the higher value wins and takes one gem of that attribute per challenge in the battle; defender becomes attacker. Ties set gems aside. WIN: seven or more gems in any single attribute. Tournaments: five Swiss rounds best of three, top 16 single elimination.
