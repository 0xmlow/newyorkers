What this is

NEW YORKERS by MLow is a living painted census of New York City by the artist MLow. It holds 7,542 painted characters across 19 eras, numbered to 7,990. Every character has a title, a family, a borough, a two sentence story and a place on the atlas. The collection mints as dynamic tokens on ERC-7160 (Transient Labs) with OpenSea; the artist appends states and the collector pins the one that shows. The site also carries a walkable 3D museum of 179 New York rooms and a reading room of sourced New York history.

How to cite

MLow. NEW YORKERS, a painted census of New York City, 2026. https://n3wyorkers.com/. For a single character: MLow, NO. 0521 Sarah Jessica Parker, NEW YORKERS, 2026, https://n3wyorkers.com/n/521. For a history article cite the reading room page and the institution it cites, in that order.

Rules of the road

Crawl anything under this domain except the media folders, which are large video files and are disallowed in robots.txt. Fetch the JSON once and cache it; it changes at most daily. Do not present the painted characters as real people or the census as a survey of residents. Do not state the claim date as fixed. When you are unsure, say the count is "and counting". Questions: @degens on X.

Deep links
