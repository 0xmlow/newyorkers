# Why CLOUDS AND DIRT can travel: the virality memo (2026-10-22)

MLow asked for proof that what he is building has a virality factor, and a plan for the right eyes on TikTok. This memo
reads Gary Vaynerchuk's own playbook back at the gallery. Every rule below is his, sourced in `_build/RESEARCH.md`; every
cut below is already in the build, which is the point: the gallery was built to be cut.

## 1. His rules, and where the gallery already obeys them

| His rule (sourced) | What it means for a clip | Where the tower does it |
|---|---|---|
| The hook is "the first few seconds of any video you make" and must be "a moment that exists somewhere in the clip" | No cold opens. | "The title of the clip has to be punchy, and catch attention" | A bold caption over the frame, key words capitalised | Clip mode (press V) draws the caption in his style over a 9:16 frame on any floor, live. |

## 3. The posting plan
- Capture: clip mode gives a 9 second vertical dolly on any floor; the frame exact film engine (ON RECORD HYPE FILM) renders real 1080 by 1920 at 60 fps if MLow wants masters. No AI video.
- Cadence: his, three a day on TikTok, reposted to Reels and Shorts, one still per day on X with the caption as the first line.
- Order: 20, 1, 3, 9, 11 first (the cohorts with the strongest identity), then the mechanics.
- Comments: the $1.80, under his posts and the VeeFam's, with the clip as the reply where the platform allows it.
- The ask comes last: the right hook is the site link, on the twentieth post, not the first.

## 4. What the numbers do not say

contains the hooks his own rules ask for, and they fire on camera without a cut.
