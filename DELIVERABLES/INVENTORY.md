## Systems

- Live New York time; sun and moon computed for Liberty Island (40.69 N, 74.04 W), Eastern time with daylight saving
- Four FLORA photographs crossfaded by real sun elevation; real moon phase disc; reflections built from the same photographs
- Daily weather from the date: clear, scattered, harbour fog, rain, snow in winter
- Calendar: mint days Mon/Wed/Fri, weekends, Spooky SZN pumpkins in October, fireworks on Genesis Block, The Memes anniversary, July 4, Merge Day, Bitcoin Whitepaper Day, New Year; gm and gn hours; card of the day; season tint
- Bloom, colour grade, vignette and grain; 9,000 wind blown grass tufts; 40 gulls; 6 sailboats; the gm blimp; traffic on the freeway; a whale every 90 seconds
- Island tour (C): 21 camera rails, the same ones the film uses
- Windows 98 interface: MEME_ISLAND.exe window, Start menu, taskbar clock, tray balloons, crash dialog
- Proof of Touching Grass receipt, postcards, paper hands dryer, WAGMI ride, OM portal, vault, six keys
- Hang your own JPGs on THE OPEN WALL (upload or drag and drop)
