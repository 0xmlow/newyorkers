# I BUILT AN ISLAND OUT OF 527 MEMES

### All 527 cards of The Memes by 6529, 144 NEW YORKERS, 66 sculptures, a lighthouse shaped like a hardware wallet and a Mona Lisa locked in a bank. You can walk every inch of it.

---

## THE PITCH, IF YOU CAN CALL IT THAT

Some people make a gallery. I made an island.

MEME ISLAND is a walkable 3D world built in three.js, by me and Claude, as part of my virtual architect practice. It hangs every one of the 527 cards of The Memes by 6529 (archive snapshot, July 2026) across 18 districts, sorted by meme pillar. It hangs 144 of my NEW YORKERS beside them. It has 66 sculptures, a ferris wheel, a factory, a bank, a bunker, a freeway, a beach, a bodega and a 50 metre statue standing in the harbour.

It is absurd. It is trolling. It is also the most sincere thing I have built, and I will get to that.

The house design language is the same one I use for every room I build: Daniel Arsham fictional archaeology, Zaha Hadid curves, Frank Lloyd Wright compression and release, and Virgil Abloh quotation mark labels in safety orange. Put those four in a room with 527 memes and this is what happens.

## THE FLOOR IS A MOUNTAIN

The first thing you see is THE FLOOR. All 527 cards, stacked into a mountain by floor price. You can climb it. People will.

The plaque at the bottom reads:

> "THE FLOOR IS A SOCIAL CONSTRUCT. WATCH YOUR STEP. IT HAS REPRICED."

That is the tone for the rest of the island. Every joke is placed on purpose.

## THE LIGHTHOUSE IS A HARDWARE WALLET

The lighthouse is 48 metres tall and it is a hardware wallet. The door is the USB port. The screen at the top says CONFIRM ON DEVICE. The plaque says:

> "SELF CUSTODY. PLEASE USE THE OTHER DOOR."

There is also an Open Metaverse portal somewhere on the island. Step through it and it drops you on the lighthouse roof. I will not say where it is.

## THE VAULT SAYS NO.

The vault opens for six hidden keys and nothing else. Grumpy Cat stands at the door and says NO.

Inside is the painting I made for @punk6529 as his NEW YORKERS honorary: "Cards on the Bridge". Find all six keys and you get to see it. That is the whole reward. It is a good reward.

## THE FIRST NATIONAL BANK OF JPGS

Next door is the First National Bank of JPGs. Agents in suits walk the floor stealing cards off the plinths. They keep doing it until you catch them.

The Mona Lisa is locked inside. Of course she is. Where else would a bank keep a JPG.

## THE BRAIN, THE WHEEL, THE EXIT

The BRAIN is a pink dome you can walk into. Inside, terminals give you advice. The advice contradicts itself. Just like the timeline.

Then there is the WAGMI ferris wheel, built out of cards, and you can ride it. At the very top it says STILL EARLY. Nyan Cat orbits the whole thing forever.

Next to it is the NGMI EXIT. It leads back to friends.

## SURVIVE, SEIZE, BUIDL

The SURVIVE bunker holds 105 cards and a calcified bear. This Is Fine stands right behind the bear, because of course he does.

The MEMES OF PRODUCTION factory has a SEIZE lever. Pull it and cards rain from the ceiling.

BUIDL is permanently unfinished. It has an OPEN WALL where visitors hang their own JPGs. The permit on the fence reads:

> "BUIDL PERMIT PENDING SINCE GENESIS."

## ZERO GAS. EMOTIONAL TOLLS APPLY.

The Freedom to Transact freeway runs past 71 card billboards to a zero gas toll booth. Rick Astley stands at the booth. It prints you a receipt for 0.0000 ETH.

Keep walking and you reach Summer.jpg, a boardwalk where it is always noon. There is a TAP fountain, the Digital Rights Charter monolith, GM BEACH, the Census Ferry, and the 6529 BODEGA, which has a bodega cat and Kermit sipping tea.

## THE COLOSSUS OF 6529

Out in the water stands the Colossus of 6529: a 50 metre voxel statue of the punk6529 honorary figure, raising a hardware wallet like a torch.

Type 6529 and it fires laser eyes.

## THINGS YOU WILL FIND IF YOU POKE AT IT

- Type gm and the island replays today's real sunrise. Every resident answers gm.
- Type gn and the island answers: WE DON'T SAY THAT HERE.
- There is a rug on the beach. It rugs you.
- Right click the ocean and you get scolded. The sign warned you: "PLEASE DO NOT RIGHT CLICK THE OCEAN."
- Stand still long enough and you earn TDH, total days held.
- Stand in the grass ring for 10 seconds and you get a Proof of Touching Grass receipt.
- There is a paper hands dryer. There is a whale alert.
- The Konami code tilts the whole island 3 percent.

## THE ISLAND KEEPS REAL TIME

This part comes from my Marfa Light project, and it is my favourite thing on the island.

The sun and moon are computed for Liberty Island on real New York time. Four AI photographs of the harbour, dawn, noon, golden hour and night with stars, crossfade by where the sun actually is right now. A real moon phase hangs in the sky beside an evil eye moon.

There is daily weather: clear, scattered, harbour fog, rain, and snow in winter.

There is a calendar. On Monday, Wednesday and Friday the factory belt speeds up, because those are the island's mint days. Pumpkins show up all through Spooky SZN in October. Fireworks go off on Bitcoin Whitepaper Day, Genesis Block day, The Memes anniversary on June 9, July 4th and New Year. Residents say gm in the morning and gn late at night. A new card of the day hangs at midnight.

You could visit twice in one day and see two different islands.

## IT RUNS ON WINDOWS 98

The interface is a Windows 98 desktop. The island opens in a MEME_ISLAND.exe window. The Start menu lists every place. The taskbar tray keeps the New York clock and pops balloon notifications at you.

It felt right. The internet that made memes deserves the desktop it grew up on.

## THE SINCERE PART

Under all the trolling, this is a love letter.

The Memes by 6529 carries the biggest ideas in this space: self custody, freedom to transact, an open metaverse. I wanted to build the place those ideas would live if they were a place. A real island, with real weather, real sunrises and real people walking around in it.

The 144 NEW YORKERS on the walls are the people of this culture. A bodega clerk who mints from a phone. A man who keeps his seed phrase stamped in steel. A degen who buys the top and holds to the bottom. They come from Eras XVIII and XIX of the NEW YORKERS census, and they belong on this island more than anyone.

One plaque says it plainest:

> "MLOW PAINTED THE PEOPLE. THE PEOPLE BUILT THE PLACE."

And for anyone who needs to hear it twice:

> "THIS FRAME IS NOT THE OWNER."
