THE WRAPPED WING

The museum closed at nine. You are holding the gun.

The wrap crew came in through the gift shop and got everything: the whale, the gods, the parade horse, the sphinxes, the moon. Walk the galleries after closing with the gun. The guards only move while you are not looking at them. The pool remembers the sphinxes plain. One painting is scattered across the court until you stand on the gold star. The audio guide lies. Touch anything in the American wing and the alarm has opinions. Look at the duck in the gift shop too long and it costs more. On the roof the moon is bigger near the skyline, and nobody knows why. Then go down to the lab, where the gun unwraps, and one of the originals was never original.
