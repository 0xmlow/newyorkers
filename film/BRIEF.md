# BREAK GLASS, the hype film

**The one sentence:** there is a New Yorker behind the glass, and you have to break it to meet them.

- **Audience and channel:** collectors on X and the mint page. Muted autoplay first: every beat reads without sound.
- **Duration and master:** 44 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264, -14 LUFS. A 1080x1080 square cut for the feed.
- **Reference style:** a sneaker drop cold open (Nike, "Dream Crazy" pacing) crossed with a fire drill PSA: macro product shots cut on the beat, one slow motion money shot, a slot machine montage.
- **Hero moment:** 12.0 s, the third hit. One beat of silence, then the glass goes in slow motion and the fire jets go up.
- **Engine:** the piece itself, frame exact (virtual clock, `_engine/rig.py`), cameras and type from `_engine/film.js`, score synthesised in `_engine/score.py`. No generation credits, no stock.
- **Brand:** no dashes, MLow, no supply total (the 111 and 163 are what is inside this one piece, from the mint kit), no dates, no price.
