A tower for the man who day trades attention, with the dirt in the basement and the clouds on the roof. Flip a garage sale find on the folding table. Taste four wines at the Wine Library desk and the whole room changes colour. Jab the heavy bag three times, then throw the hook. Stand in the ring light on floor 25 and watch sixty four screens cut you into content. Record six seconds of yourself in the studio and meet your loop. Walk the twelve and a half ingredients under his portrait on Park Avenue and face the mirror of kind candor. Ride the carousel of friends and wait eleven seconds on the Patient Panda. Fill an arena by walking. Buy attention while it is underpriced. Throw a football through the uprights from the owner's box. Then stand on a star on the roof and read the sky.
