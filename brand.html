05 · The rules

Nine rules that keep it ours.

  • No dashes. Never an em dash or an en dash in anything public. Commas, colons, periods.
  • Two sentences. A character description is two sentences and the second one lands bare.
  • Numbers are computed, never typed. Every count on the site comes from the data. Quote the live number and say "and counting".
  • The claim is open. THE CENSUS RELEASE mints on OpenSea from September 23, 2026 at 11:11 AM ET to October 23, 2026 at 11:11 AM ET, at 0.0042 ETH, about $11 when it opened. Quote the price in ETH, never in dollars: the dollar figure moves. Quote the window, never a sellout.
  • No hype. No "iconic", "legendary", "stunning". No rocket emojis. Reverent pieces never get a hype format.
  • Real people are handled with care. Era I paints public figures as legends; no private individual is painted without consent, and gifts go to the person first.
  • The eye never blinks. Stills keep the eye still. Motion is for the loops.
  • It is art. Not an investment, not a survey, not a photograph. Say what it is. Never call the work a product.
