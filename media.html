CLOUDS AND DIRT

The tower, the skate film·2:00

Twenty five floors built for Gary Vaynerchuk, skated top to bottom by twenty five unlikely riders: a yellow cab out of the lobby, the Kid in the dirt, the Lucky Black Cat over the VeeCon stage, a panda in the Spectacular vault, a worm on Burn Island, a pig that flips the gym at half court. Every floor plays its own song, every floor's trick goes off on the beat, and all twenty five play at once before the Kid lands on the roof among the words in the sky.
