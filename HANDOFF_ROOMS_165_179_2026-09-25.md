# Handoff: rooms 165 to 179, built 2026-09-25

Read this if you are picking up THE MUSEUM after the fifteen room batch of
2026-09-25. Everything below is committed in `NEW YORKERS SITE` (a local git repo
with no remote; the deploy is the push). Nothing is deployed.

## 1. What happened, in order

1. **Fifteen places chosen against the 164 that existed.** The Wonder Wheel is
   already in `coney`, Grand Army Plaza in `brooklyncentral`, Jackson Heights in
   `laundry`, so those were dropped. The fifteen: the City Hall loop, the gold
   vault, Union Square, Bryant Park, the Little Red Lighthouse, Four Freedoms
   Park, the sea lion pool, the Delacorte at night, the Cherry Esplanade, the
   Promenade, Astor Place, the Queens Night Market, Dyker Heights, Hunts Point,
   Columbus Park.
2. **Scaffold first.** Placeholders in `src/rooms/v15.ts` to `v22.ts`, registered
   in `v0.ts`, curated in `data.ts`, checks assert 179, committed before any
   builder started, so every builder's bundle compiled from minute one.
3. **The brief.** `_build/museum/NEW_ROOMS_165_179_BRIEF.md`: one paragraph per
   room with its hero, its interaction and where the art honestly hangs; two rules
   on top of the standard (real New York time visibly, one thing the visitor can
   do); census wall starts 250 to 1650 in steps of 100; tags n15 to n22, ports
   9415 to 9422.
4. **Four builders at a time, two waves.** One file each. The lead looked at every
   spawn shot and sent two rooms back once each (a lamp post on the Promenade's
   centre line, a blooming trailer at the Hunts Point spawn). One commit per file
   with the reasoning.
5. **The Blender life kit.** `_build/museum/blender/build_life_kit.py`: sea lion
   (hauled out and swimming), koi, turtle, swan, pigeon, gull, dog, nutcracker, as
   flat material GLBs in `assets/museum/props`. Reviewed on the contact sheet; the
   sea lion's taper and the nutcracker's arms were wrong the first time.
6. **Engine fixes.** The desktop debug overlay read `calls 1 · tris 0k` under the
   fx composer; `main.ts` now resets `renderer.info` once per frame so the count
   is the whole frame. The four headless runners delete their Chrome profiles on
   exit (184 of them once held 8 GB).
7. **Export, thumbs, build.** `MUSEUM EXPORTS/2026-09-25/` holds the fifteen GLBs
   (36 to 65 MB), posters and records; thumbs cut; `build_all.sh` run.
8. **Mint kit.** `MUSEUM EXPORTS/2026-09-25 MINT/` links today's exports;
   `mint_gifs.py` and `mint_network.py --rooms <the fifteen>` write
   `MUSEUM MINT NETWORK 2026-09-25/` with tokens, metadata, web GLBs and previews
   for the fifteen. See section 4 for what that kit is and is not.
9. **Skill.** `virtual-architect` v3.5 in `SKILL UPDATES 2026-09-25/` and the root
   `virtual-architect.skill`; the installed copy is the claude.ai upload, so MLow
   re-uploads it. `~/.claude/skills/3d-gallery-marketplace/SKILL.md` gained a
   shared three.js practice section. 3D asset prompts per room are in
   `MUSEUM ROOMS 165-179 2026-09-25/3D_ASSET_PROMPTS.md`.

## 3. Not verified, plainly

- No room was walked by a person in a live browser. Every interaction was
  exercised headless with the camera placed by `--at` and probed with `--eval`.
- Real clock moments (the half hour at the zoo, the quarter hour on 84th Street,
  20:00 at the Delacorte, noon at Union Square) were seen only through `?hour=`.
- Frame rate on a real phone was not measured; the phone overlay reads 50 to 135
  draw calls per room.
- Draw calls were measured three different ways by the builders (scene traversal,
  per pass averages, the new whole frame overlay); the numbers in the commit
  messages say which. Compare rooms on the whole frame overlay from now on.
- The mint kit for the fifteen is partial by design (section 4).

## 4. The mint kit, what it is and is not

`mint_network.py` builds a networked kit: every room a token, joined by doors.
Today's run was given only the fifteen new exports, so `MUSEUM MINT NETWORK
2026-09-25/` carries tokens, metadata, compressed web GLBs and previews for the
fifteen, and its lobby and ring doors only know those fifteen. The full 179 room
network needs a same day export of every room (about 8 GB of GLB, more than the
disk had free today) and the previews for all of them (about an hour). The
commands are in `_build/museum/mint/00_START_HERE.md`. The raw GLBs, posters and
records for the fifteen are in `MUSEUM EXPORTS/2026-09-25/` and can be minted one
by one with the older `mint_kit.py` flow or any viewer.
