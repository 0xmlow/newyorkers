How a memory is chosen

One nomination is picked every Sunday. The New Yorker in it is painted on Monday and posted Tuesday at 11:11 AM with the nominator credited by alias. The subject is never tagged and never named beyond what you wrote.

You keep your story and your photo. The painting is a gift: it comes to you as a share card and a Resident Card, it is never sold and never used in an advertisement. If a story or a likeness is ever used commercially, that is a separate agreement in writing, signed by the person it belongs to.

Photos are references for the painting only and are deleted after the piece is made. To withdraw a story at any time, reply to the email you receive or write to the address in the footer.
