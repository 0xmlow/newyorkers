# Image-first asset production

Tool: built-in ImageGen, four calls, October 18, 2026. Generated first, before room implementation. Original PNG outputs are in public/assets/generated. Generation did not alter collection originals.

1. **contour.png**. Production material: square aged deep-navy painted cast iron/plaster, physically painted liquid topographic veins in cobalt, cyan, copper and bone, gravity drips, worn grain. Orthographic scan, neutral illumination, no lettering. Used on real columns, portals, cases, and planters. No measured normal or height data is claimed.
2. **wheatpaste.png**. Broad straight-on layered municipal wheatpaste with torn forms, blue paint rivers, pigeon woodcut insignia and a watchful six-petal eye. Requested phrases: “UNLICENSED MIRACLE”, “RENT DUE IN ANOTHER DIMENSION”, “PIGEON JURY DUTY”, “NEW YORKERS”. Used as environmental paper rather than collection art.
3. **vista.png**. Wide impossible New York night skyline, tenements, fire escapes and water towers bending overhead, dark cobalt, amber windows, wet cyan, distant uncanny eye flower. Intended as a protected distant vista behind modeled architecture. No foreground people, logos or text.
4. **vista-depth.png**. Edit/reference input: vista.png. Matching grayscale inverse-depth estimate preserving composition and aspect ratio. Bright near terrace, mid-gray facade wings, dark distant center, black sky; no material lighting or decorative marks. This is authored estimated depth, not measured geometry.

The exact generation requests are preserved in prompts.json. The JPEG vista is only a delivery encoding of the original PNG. All image roles, output hashes and dimensions are recorded in asset-manifest.json.
