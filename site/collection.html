NEW YORKERS // BY MLOW. Forty-four works preserved at the end of everything.

Materials and asset credits

ARCHIVE 930

The Roll Down Gate

The gate rolls down at ten and the night shift of painters clocks in. One masterpiece burner over years of tags, the streetlight raking every ridge

ARCHIVE 1599

The Night Vet

The 3am animal ER, a cat's paw bandaged while its human cries with relief. One recovering pigeon in a shoebox marked with a heart, mercy running the night shift

ARCHIVE 2627

The Steps Lunch

A hundred sandwiches at the feet of five thousand years of art. The pigeons work the crowd like docents collecting dues

ARCHIVE 2101

The Fool

He steps off the bus with one suitcase and total faith, a bodega cat at his heels. The skyline is the cliff he cannot see, and he will survive it

ARCHIVE 3200

The Steps

A thousand cameras fire and she turns around to see the crowd instead. The museum steps, limestone gold, a gown blooming all the way down

ARCHIVE 4577

The Docent of Forgotten Donors

She reads the small brass names aloud and the named arrive. A monthly tour for four hundred generosities, her voice the only thing keeping them warm

ARCHIVE 5697

Vladan Novotny, a night nurse holding a floor alone

Holding an entire floor alone is not heroism, it is a staffing decision. Bed to chair, one careful lift, a phone face down on the tray

ARCHIVE 5712

Leah Teitelbaum, a night companion paid to simply be awake in a house

Being awake is the entire job, and it is harder than it sounds. Four in the morning, a notebook of times and doses, a strange lamp

ARCHIVE 5715

Fitzroy Bonaparte, a night nurse holding a floor alone

Endurance keeps getting mistaken for a plan on the night shift. Two minutes in the stairwell, hands cracked, a tube of cream in the pocket

ARCHIVE 5724

Choden Sarmiento, a respite worker who gets four hours to be a person

Four hours is what the city allocates for a family to breathe. Called the daughter by a neighbour, she nods and picks up the transfer belt

ARCHIVE 5736

Halima Achebe, a dementia care specialist who plays one song every day

Music reaches a place the medication has never once found. She sings the same song quietly into a room with no answer

ARCHIVE 5739

Arjun Rahman, a doula who has delivered on four bathroom floors

Four bathroom floors, four first breaths, and no line on any certificate. Mistaken for a cousin at the door, he lets it stand and carries the bag

ARCHIVE 548

Pizza Rat

ARCHIVE 550

Bodega Cat

ARCHIVE 779

ARCHIVE 780

The Rainbow Bagel

ARCHIVE 818

ARCHIVE 1367

ARCHIVE 1690
