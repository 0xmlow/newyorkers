Made of memories.

Artworks, brand marks and ten architectural/sculptural GLB assets come from the NEW YORKERS // BY MLow project library. Original artwork files are preserved; display copies and product labels are used here.

Three material textures were created specifically for this world with OpenAI ImageGen: aged ceramic, weathered brick and teal enamel. These are authored material studies, not scans of an existing shop.

Eight bakery and deli models are from Kenney’s Food Kit, used under CC0. Included license.

Three.js powers the real-time world under its MIT license. Architecture, narrative puzzles, room designs, shader effects and interactive systems were authored for this fictional exhibition.
