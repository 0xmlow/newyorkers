A building full of stories.

Original artwork and brand marks: MLow / NEW YORKERS. Display copies preserve source proportions and identifiers. Full provenance is recorded in assets/art.json.

This venue is a fictional New York industrial loft, not a measured reconstruction or an actual property development. All tenant records, sales language and redevelopment events are invented for this exhibition.

Scanned PBR materials from Old Wood Floor, Brown Brick 02, and Concrete Floor 02, courtesy of Poly Haven under CC0. Color, normal, roughness, ambient-occlusion and height maps are used. Height data drives parallax relief shading; this is not a 3D scan of the building. Architecture and optical installations are original procedural geometry. The bottomless shaft uses an actual floor opening and a planar mirror; the other illusions use separated shapes and forced perspective.

Optical study 07, The Rebrand, is painted the way Felice Varini paints buildings: one flat image projected from a single viewpoint onto every surface it reaches. Here a projector camera renders a depth map of the working floor, so the paint lands only where it would land on the real thing.
