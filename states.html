Friday State.

A NEW YORKER on a token is a stack of finished states, and the stack grows. New states land on Fridays. This page is the schedule.

Dates are the artist's published intent and can move. Nothing already on a token is ever removed.

How pinning works

A Keystone is an ERC-7160 token on Transient Labs: one token, a stack of finished states, and the artist can append to the stack after you own it.

The holder pins the state that shows, from their own wallet on transient.xyz, and can move the pin any time; a state is never taken off the stack.

Census New Yorkers mint on OpenSea as ERC-721 tokens that are dynamic by metadata: the stack of states lives in each token and its own viewer, so the same Fridays reach them.
