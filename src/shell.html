THE PIGEONHOLE

A museum inside a pigeon, inside a bodega, inside a dream you are having on the last train home.
IThe dream IIThe bodega IIIThe pigeon IVThe museum

Twelve rooms at four scales. Each room is inside something in the room before it, and the last one holds the first. Fall into a pigeon's eye. Open a cooler that goes on for a mile. Find yourself, very small, in a glass case. Every room has a trick that only works in a browser.

WASD or arrows to walk · mouse or drag to look · click anything · I for the index of every piece · [ and ] change rooms
On a phone: left thumb walks, right thumb looks, tap anything.
