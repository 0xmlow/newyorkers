LIVE FROM THE SEWERS

You fell in. Everybody falls in.

Fall through the manhole and walk your shadow through customs: it is approved, you are not. Push the slice up the stairs to Pizza Rat's shrine and watch the stairs get longer. Pull the chain in the goldfish Upstate Farm and the room fills past your eyes. Pull the 1935 clipping and the gator wakes; hold still by the water and something surfaces. Light six votives in the fatberg. Drop one sesame seed and crash the Crumb Exchange. Ring the left earbud and every rat leaves. Pump the bellows until the 1870 train is cancelled due to atmosphere. Turn the spa dial from bagel to lawsuit. Vote until the agenda returns to item one. Cross the bodega border only while the cat is not looking. Then pull the plunger and go up one problem.
