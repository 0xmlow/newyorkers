10 · The museum
Walk the city as a gallery. Sixty one rooms, every New Yorker hung where they were counted, the light following the New York clock.

A Three.js museum built room by room: the Bowery after hours, the Guggenheim ramp, the Chrysler lobby under its crown, the Vessel, the Cyclone, the crown of the Statue, the Panorama, a Bushwick rooftop, the nave of St. John the Divine. Every interior comes with its street and its skyline. Each room hangs the characters the census recorded at that place first, then any era, family or set you choose. The light follows the New York clock and the hang reshuffles daily. Any room exports as a GLB. Click a work and the record opens.
