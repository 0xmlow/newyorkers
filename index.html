01 · The thesis

Every New Yorker gets a portrait. Even the villains.

The work has run on more than 5,000 New York City taxis and been shown on the streets of ten cities in ten countries. A portrait of an entire city, one character at a time, painted by a city that is watching back. The eye flowers are the proof: when a flower with a human iris blooms in a frame, this moment was witnessed, this person mattered, somebody was watching with love.

02 · The three marks

The Eye Flower

The city's attention made visible. It blooms wherever a moment is truly witnessed. Its iris is human because the city's eyes are its people.

Protection. It watches so you can stop watching your back. It never moves and never blinks in stills. It is MLow's sovereign mark.

The Sigil

10 · The museum
Walk the city as a gallery. One hundred and eleven rooms, every New Yorker hung where they were counted, the light following the New York clock.

A Three.js museum built room by room: the Bowery after hours, the Guggenheim ramp, the Chrysler lobby under its crown, the Vessel, the Cyclone, the crown of the Statue, Studio 8H, the Met's Great Hall, the whale at Natural History, thirty art museums and institutions across the five boroughs, the nave of St. John the Divine. Every interior comes with its street and its skyline. Each room hangs the characters the census recorded at that place first, then any era, family or set you choose. The light follows the New York clock and the hang reshuffles daily. Any room exports as a GLB. Click a work and the record opens.
