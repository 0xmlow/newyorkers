# MLOW INTERNATIONAL walk: THE BIG UPGRADE (2026-11-24)

Brief: `NEW YORKERS SITE/_build/GALLERY_UPGRADE_2026-11-23.md`. Everything lives in `site/upgrade.js`, loaded by
`site/main.js` after the terminal is built; if it throws, the terminal opens as it was. The baked set
(`airport.glb` and its four lightmaps) is untouched.

Switches: `?up=0` (the terminal as it was, used for every before shot), `?look=0`, `?scan=0`, `?props=0`, `?built=0`.
Harness: `window.__walk.up()` returns the upgrade (`loaded`, `probes`, `illusions`).

## What it does

- **scan**: six Poly Haven CC0 sets in `site/assets/tex/` (`tools/polyhaven_tex.py` fetches them). The bake holds
  colour times light, so a scan's luminance (mean rescaled to mid grey) multiplies over the bake in world metres,
  triplanar, with a slow second sample so tiles never repeat. Up, down and side faces of one baked material can take
  different scans (the plaza paving is the same material as the concourse ceiling). Interior floors (`terrazzo`,
  `liner_blue`) reflect through the scan's normal and roughness maps.
- **look** (desktop only): a reflection probe per hall (rotunda, concourse, hub, arrivals) taken after the models land
  and again at 4 s; the floors and the models inside take the hall's probe. The frame renders into a 4x MSAA half float
  target, then a half size AO pass (12 samples, faded out past 25 to 60 m because the bake already holds large scale
  occlusion), then one grade pass: ACES (the old renderer used AgX), cool shadows, warm highlights, a soft S curve,
  vignette, grain. The interior and exterior exposure switch in main.js still drives it.
- **props**: 30 box cars become yellow cabs (five of our Tripo cab models, one per car colour set, instanced at each
  car's own matrix); the pink and green ball trees become `tree_cherry` (desktop only; phones keep the balls); the
  station clock hangs over the pupil under the dome; stanchion queues with belts at the four check in desks; palms
  round the drum and along the concourse; benches down the concourse and in the gate pods; luggage beside about half the
  travellers and riding both carousels (the rings turn); the globe at arrivals; Poly Haven street lamps on the drop off
  kerb; Poly Haven jersey barriers along the north apron edge; a baggage train of four dollies and a tug. Every
  placement is checked against the floor (nothing standing within the footprint) and the travellers; footprint boxes
  join the collision BVH once the models land (`addSolids` in main.js).
- **built**: oak baffles down both sides of the concourse skylight, downlights in the concourse, the hub and arrivals;
  signs in Archivo Expanded and JetBrains Mono (concourse wayfinding, four check in signs, BAGGAGE 1 and 2 totems, the
  DEPARTURES sign on the drop off median).
- **illusions**, one per hall, all anamorphic (`anamorph()`: a small real 3D scene rendered from one standpoint and
  projected back onto the flat floor from the same point): WELCOME TO NEW YORK block letters standing on the rotunda
  floor, read from the spawn; a floating zebra crossing over the drop off lane (mark on the median); a shaft through
  the concourse floor onto open sky (mark at x -3.4, z -61.6); STAND B4 standing on the apron, read from the apron spot;
  in the hub, the evil eye assembled from 81 floating shards only from the mark in the neck (z -172); in arrivals, a
  peripheral drift rug (still, seems to turn) round the globe. The yellow rings are the marks.
- **Firefly**: `assets/ff/arrivals_mural.webp` (queued) shows as a frieze over the census works on the back wall of
  arrivals once the file lands. A 3D PAID OK airliner is queued for the apron.
