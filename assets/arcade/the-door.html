How it works
  1. Five doors. Each has hidden rules about what you are wearing.
  2. You see the people ahead of you, their full fit, and whether they got in.

  4. Build your fit from four slots. Every item has a vibe and a flex rating, and the door cares about both.
  5. Two attempts per door. On a denial he names one thing that was wrong.
  6. Everyone in the world gets the same night. It resets at midnight.
