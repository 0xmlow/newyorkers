THE
LAST TRAIN

2am at Bedford Av. Every time somebody squeezes on, the doors bounce open and the conductor waits a little less. Last one through takes the night's fares.

How it works
  1. You get three tickets. Each one puts you on the train and reopens the doors.
  2. Every fare splits  . Board early and you earn from everyone who gets on after you.
  3. You cannot push on twice in a row. While you are the last one on, there is nothing to do but watch the clock.
  4. The doors lock somewhere under five and a half seconds, and you are never told exactly where. Cutting it fine is a gamble, not a technique.
  5. The platform empties as riders run out of nerve and tickets. Read the crowd.
Free to play, and staying that way

Tickets are granted, never sold, and the pot is fares, not money. Nothing here can be bought and nothing pays out. The paid path exists in the code and is switched off.
