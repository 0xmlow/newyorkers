# MLow Impermanent Vision

## Visual thesis

Build worlds where systems become landscapes and landscapes become conscious. Let paint reveal the hidden current beneath architecture, technology, memory, and power.

The image should feel like reality has been cut open while the witness remains calm enough to look inside

## Spatial grammar

Use thresholds as the primary engine:

* doorways that open into incompatible environments
* corridors whose walls behave like liquid stone
* bridges and tunnels treated as portals
* stairs that enter voids, fields, or oversized botanical forms
* distant openings that pull contour lines toward a vanishing point
* monumental faces, flowers, skulls, or eyes that become inhabitable space

Favor a lone figure seen from behind, in profile, or in dark silhouette. Keep the figure grounded and human while the environment carries the transformation.

## Surface grammar

Layer photoreal or graphic forms with:

* fine topographic contour lines
* fluid acrylic marbling
* luminous energy veins
* vertical paint drips and gravity traces
* carved relief edges
* dense hand worked filaments
* black negative space with selective electric illumination

Make the line field respond to volume. Lines should bend around faces, trees, steel, petals, hills, and portals.

## Open Eye Flowers

Build the flower from five to seven broad white or bone petals around a realistic blue iris and black pupil. Allow faint cobalt, copper, or gray marbling through the petals.

Use one dominant flower for confrontation or revelation.

Use two to five smaller flowers as witnesses embedded in architecture, smoke, trees, circuitry, or darkness.

Never scatter flowers as decoration. Each flower watches something.

## Palette

Anchor most images in:

* Ink void: `#02040C`
* Midnight navy: `#06163A`
* Electric cobalt: `#2962FF`
* Luminous cyan: `#00E5FF`
* Deep violet: `#6F23A8`
* Hot pink: `#FF2E63`
* Bone white: `#F0F4F8`
* Restrained copper: `#BE854C`

Use acid yellow, orange, and green only as brief signals. Do not distribute every hue evenly.

## Density and hierarchy

Keep one dominant focal structure, one human scale witness, and one path for the eye.

Let high density collect around portals, eyes, and power centers. Allow quieter dark fields to keep the image breathable.

## Intensity levels

### Whisper

Apply the language to roughly one fifth of the image. Use edge invasions, a small Open Eye Flower, restrained contour highlights, and minimal drips.

### Bloom

Apply the language to roughly two fifths of the image. Let architecture and atmosphere partially liquefy. Use one visible threshold and one or more watchful flowers.

### Melt

Apply the language to more than half of the image. Let terrain, sky, and architecture merge while preserving the principal subject, identity, and intended action.

## Failure modes

Reject results when:

* the effect reads as a generic psychedelic filter
* the palette becomes evenly rainbow
* contour lines ignore form
* the flower becomes cute or decorative
* the subject loses identity or anatomy
* paint is only an overlay with no material relationship to the scene
* every region has equal detail
* text becomes generated noise
