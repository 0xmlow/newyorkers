BIGGER ON THE INSIDE

Space is the one luxury in New York. Online it is free

New York has everything except room. So here is one block with more of it than the buildings could ever hold. The bodega has one aisle and it goes back about a mile. The walk-up only goes up, and every landing is the fifth floor. Apartment 5A is nine hundred square feet and looks it from exactly one spot. The dryers at the laundromat look into the other rooms, and the big one at the back is a tunnel. The train rolls, the riders do not mind, and on the roof the city keeps going, up and over your head. Walk off the end of the street and you are back at the start.
