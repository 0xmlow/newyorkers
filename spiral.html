THE SPIRAL

Wright built the Guggenheim as a ramp. A ramp is a marble run. Walk in under the low door, look up through every tournament Bryan Brinkman's Marble Run has ever finished, one turn each, and climb. Every race painting hangs in bracket order, every champion has a bay, and the races nobody painted yet hang as wet tape with their seeds. The pennants in the void are the champions, bottom to top. When a race goes off on marblerun.fun, five marbles drop from the glass at the top and run the gutter through every run to the cups. Press R and ride one down. Then pick one of Bryan's hundred marbles and play: RINGER on the void floor, THE DROP from the glass, GUTTER DASH down the whole tower, SKEE in the paddock. Seventeen of Bryan's own works are hidden in the building, from NimBuds under the dome to five Lost Marbles. Art, not an investment. Not a betting product.
