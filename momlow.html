A walkable gallery by MLow

MoMLOW

MLow's first virtual museum was a MoMA for his own collection, with empty frames you filled by hand. This is the real MoMA plan rebuilt for NEW YORKERS: the lobby runs through the block, the atrium opens to the skylight with Liberty standing in it, the galleries ring the void floor by floor, and a curator that learned what MLow likes hung every room and refused every soft painting. Press C in any room for its note. Ride the lift. Cross the bridge. Out back, on the garden's north wall, every New Yorker anyone has collected hangs in the order it happened. Admission is free. It always was.
