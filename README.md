# Barney Greengrass, the room

Room 123 of THE MUSEUM on [n3wyorkers.com](https://n3wyorkers.com/museum#room=barney): the Sturgeon King's shop
at 541 Amsterdam Avenue, walkable, with MLow's merch for the store on its walls, his characters at the counter and
the regulars of the census hung on the New Orleans wallpaper. Built by MLow, 2026-09-13 to 2026-10-12.

This branch is the room's source and assets, lifted from the site repository. It is not a standalone page: the room
is one entry in the museum's TypeScript engine, bundled with the other 182 rooms into `assets/museum/museum.js` on
the site. Walk it at the link above.

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `_build/museum/src/rooms/z.ts` | The room (`barney`), beside Katz's: the model, the mirror, the wallpaper, the merch hang, the signs, the eggs, the people |
| `_build/museum/src/rooms/barney_art.ts` | The hang manifest, generated by `barney_art.py` |

| `_build/museum/barney_mirror.py` | Mirrors the front room of MLow's Blender model to the real layout, inside the GLB |

| `model/barney_greengrass_2026-09-13.glb` | The shop as modelled, untouched |

| `assets/museum/props/barney_bagel.glb` | A bagel and lox plate, Trellis image to 3D from a GPT concept |

The real layout, from first hand accounts: the deli case on the right as you come in with the chalkboards behind it,
a few formica tables in front, the bakery shelves opposite, the dining room through a doorway on the left, papered
with New Orleans since the 1950s. Cash only. No reservations. Closed Mondays.

NEW YORKERS is a census by MLow. The art is his. The store is the Greengrass family's, since 1908.
