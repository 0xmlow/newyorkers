# TEN STYLES. ONE CITY.: ten artists, forty two rooms

A walkable, photoreal three.js gallery by MLow, built with NEW YORKERS. Ten galleries, one for each artist MLow
restyled his New Yorkers through (the Krea 3 style passes of 2026-10-14): Yayoi Kusama, Jean-Michel Basquiat,
Keith Haring, Hokusai, Roy Lichtenstein, Salvador Dali, Andy Warhol, Vincent van Gogh, Pablo Picasso and Banksy.
Each gallery is three rooms: **A** rebuilds a real moment that artist or the art had in New York, **B** wraps a real
part of the city in the art, as if it had been repainted overnight, and **C, THE SHOW**, rebuilds a real New York exhibition
of the artist as a hall whose job is the hang. Every room opens onto a FLORA plate rebuilt as
3D from a depth map, hangs MLow's census New Yorkers in that artist's style (twelve in A and B, 26 in C, 520 in all, dealt by
`_build/art.py`, neighbourhood first; the vault walls in C hold all 2,207), and has one trick you can only get by walking, standing or looking.
Everybody has an eye flower for a head.

Open it: double click `Open TEN STYLES.command` (serves `site/` on localhost:4251). Not on n3wyorkers.com yet.

### Fourth pass: the wow pass (2026-11-04)

- **THE STYLE MACHINE** (room 0, the way in): 219 New Yorkers exist in all ten styles. The big screen holds one; ten
  buttons on the console dissolve it into each artist's version along a noise edge in that artist's colour; the lever pulls
  another New Yorker; the south wall shows the same one in all ten hands at once (touch a tile to switch); YOUR WALL, eight
  gold frames, keeps whatever was on the screen when you touched it (this browser).
- **Every show hall has its own architecture** (`PARTS` in build.js, the hang follows the walls): an octagon pavilion
  round Kusama's boat, Basquiat zigzags either side of the glass trench, one Haring zigzag wall like a drawn line, a
  Hokusai S curve, a Guggenheim arc for Lichtenstein, Dali's melting wall, a four screen grid for Warhol, Picasso's six
  shattered screens, crooked Banksy thrift aisles.
- **A sculpture in every hall**: four new Tripo heroes (Dali's space elephant, Van Gogh's sunflowers, Kusama's dotted
  tentacles, a crowned skull) plus the heroes of the first pass, on plinths in the east aisles.
- **Visitors**: four people standing in front of pieces in every hall. **Title cards**: the first time you enter a gallery
  (and the machine, and Luna Luna) its name crosses the screen like a film title.

### The gallery, as a gallery (every room)

- **The card**: click any piece. It opens on the restyle, then a seam draws across it: left is MLow's census original
  (`image` from `api/pieces.json`, loaded from n3wyorkers.com), right is the restyle. Drag the seam. Arrows (or the keys) walk
  the room's wall or the vault; WALK TO IT stands you in front of the piece.
- **THE HANG** (H): every piece on every wall, by room, with thumbnails, searchable by title, number, neighbourhood or artist;
  one click walks you there.
- **TOUR** (T): glides you round the room's wall piece by piece, six seconds each; any key stops it.
- Picture lights and three line labels on all 520 pieces, A and B rooms included.
- Fixed on the way: east wall pieces, the next room signs and some boards in rooms A and B sat inside the shared wall and
  were invisible since the first build.

Eggs are counted from the source at build time (`EGG_N`): 105 on the last build.

## How it was made, and what it cost (FLORA Creative Partner workspace, project "TEN STYLES ONE CITY 2026-11-04", budget $25)

## Engine

ONLY IN NEW YORK's, forked whole (depth plates, lazy rooms, warmup, power and phone guards, skate mode, clip mode). New:
- four screen modes in the post pass, eased by `FX.dots`, `FX.facet`, `FX.swirl`, `FX.quad`; a room sets one only while you are
  in its zone and only undoes its own (`fxWhile` in build.js);
- the scene target has a stencil buffer (the infinity booth draws its lattice only where its black lining is visible);
- `anamorph()` (dots along rays from one eye), the surface projection in 2A, the barrier grid in 3B, `bwScreen()` for
  the Screen Test, the flashlight shader in 10B.

Build: `python3 _build/build.py` (or `--js`). Check: `python3 _build/check_all.py work/check` (all twenty headless, 0 errors on
the last run) and `python3 _build/tricks.py work/tricks` (each room shot from where its trick works). Perf:
`python3 _build/perf.py` (55 to 58 fps headless on this Mac, p95 16.8 ms, 16 to 21 MB to the first frame).
