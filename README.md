# ON LOCATION: New York at the Movies

A walkable three.js gallery by MLow. Eighty five scenes New York gave the movies, rebuilt as sets and recast with
NEW YORKERS: every member of the cast has an eye flower for a head, so the scenes are the films' and the people
are ours (no actor's likeness anywhere, no studio logo; the no ghost sign is an eye flower behind the bar).
Each set's walls hold twelve census New Yorkers from the block where the film was shot (`HOODS` in build.py).

Open it: double click `Open ON LOCATION.command`. On the site it is `movies.html`
(`NEW YORKERS SITE/_build/build_galleries.py` copies `site/` into `assets/movies`).

260 scene eggs (counted from build.js at build time). The engine is THE MARKS engine (`../THE MARKS GALLERY 2026-10-09`) plus a screen pass
(saturation, heat, warp, grain) that only runs when a room asks for it, a ground and move hook per room, a
field of view per room, and GLB clicks that survive late loading.

## Props: Poly Haven, CC0

116 props are Poly Haven models (polyhaven.com, CC0): street lamps, hydrants, trash cans, manholes, barriers,
crates, chandeliers, sofas, chairs, desks, a cash register, a coffee cart, binoculars, cameras, a projector,
clocks, statues and more. `python3 _build/polyhaven.py id[:tris] ...` downloads the 1k glTF, runs the
museum's prop pass (`_build/ph_pass.py`, prop_pass reading glTF) at 512 px textures into
`work/glb_pass/ph_<id>.glb` and deletes the download. `PROPS` in build.js places them per set; every model gets
a soft contact shadow. brass_pan_01, brass_pot_01, CheeseBox_01, garden_gnome and concrete_cat_statue do not
pass (Blender produces nothing), nor do fir_tree_01 and pine_tree_01, so the Copa's pots and the Rockefeller tree are drawn in code. polyhaven.py runs one asset per Blender call, and a dropped download skips that asset instead of the batch.

## Brand check

Round eight (sets 65 to 74) was made in Adobe Firefly (Gemini 3.1, Nano Banana 2) because the Krea balance ran out: plates at 21:9 and walls at 16:9, 2K, driven from the browser, and the cast reuses Tripo models already in the gallery (no new 3D). Firefly put real faces on the Godfather and Scent of a Woman leads (eye flowers painted over), a real hotel name on the Dundee wall (repainted N3W YORKERS HOTEL), and the office set's sign was repainted ATLAS INSURANCE. The office plate was first meant for The Apartment, which was already set 33: it became The Crowd (1928), whose insurance office The Apartment borrowed.

The first McMLOW'S plate and wall came back with real golden arches. The plate was refired with "no letter M, no arches" in the brief; the wall had its two logos painted over by hand with golden eyes. Look at every plate for real logos before it ships. The provider's moderation refused a man in his briefs (Birdman); a dressing gown went through. The I Am Legend plate came back with golden arches twice, even with a negative in the brief; the second one had its four logos painted over with nazar billboards by hand. The Devil Wears Prada plate had two magazine covers with human faces; eye flowers were painted over them.

## How it was made

1. Plates: Nano Banana Pro on Krea, 21:9 2K, eye level, one point perspective, with the eye flower, the N3W
   logo and a Mark as references (`work/jobs.json`). `python3 _build/fetch.py` downloads finished jobs.
2. `python3 _build/depth.py work/plates_raw/*.jpg` (Depth Anything V2 Small, local) writes plates and depth.
3. Walls: Nano Banana Pro flat elevations, `python3 _build/walls.py`.
4. Cast and props: Nano Banana Pro concepts on white (`work/concepts`), Tripo H3.1 on Krea
   (`work/glb_urls.txt`), the museum's `blender/prop_pass.py` into `work/glb_pass`, facing read off
   `_build/front_sheet.py` renders into `YAW` in main.js. Hydrant, pigeon and rat are THE MARKS heroes.
5. `python3 _build/build.py` (or `--js`), then `python3 _build/shot.py <dir> name=room=natm ...` for headless
   screenshots (it deletes its Chrome profile afterwards: each one is 50 to 100 MB).

## Not verified

- Never touched by a person: every check was headless Chrome on this Mac with scripted keys and clicks.
  Verified that way: the anamorphic title and its egg, the mirror reflection, the T. rex closing in and its egg,
  the step counter, the grate lift, the button and the giant rising, the wish, the glass floor dolly zoom.
- Not verified at all: the mirror's late reflection over a real 5 s stand, the bone fetch, the bottles in
  order, the spiral after effect egg (the 10 s stare needs a steady aim), the piano by foot, all fourteen
  eggs in one walk, the finale card.
- Phones: the touch stick is THE MARKS code, untried on a device here. The Reflector costs a second render
  in the mirror room; frame rate on a weak laptop is unmeasured.
- Round seven (sets 55 to 64): rendered headless with no console errors; the waltz fired headless (standing still is easy for a script). Not tried: catching bottles by hand, the flashlight chase in a real dark room, the Tootsie monitors' frame rate on a weak laptop (it is one extra 512 x 288 render every other frame), the Odd Couple swap by walking.
- Round eight: scripted headless checks fired the jug puzzle, the dolly zoom, the living wall, the knife, the tango, the fountain, the boat race and the parade. Not tried: walking through a smoke ring, the three minute bomb running out, the parade at a real walking pace.
- Round nine (sets 75 to 84): images from Adobe Firefly again, cast from the existing models. Scripted headless checks fired every trick: the tank, the loop door, the shell game, the SELL crash, the mail sacks, the tree, the billboard stare, the lifts, the twin, the rollerbladers. Faces painted over on the Splash, Miracle and Trading Places plates. The trading wall shows real ticker symbols as board text. Fixed an old bug: the Big room forced your size and speed back to normal from anywhere in the gallery, which cancelled The Crowd's giant and the tank's slow swim.
- Sound was not listened to.
- The plates stretch when seen from hard angles; the velvet ropes keep you about a metre back.
- Spend: about 36 Nano Banana Pro images (roughly $5 at $0.15) and 15 Tripo H3.1 runs, which Krea does not price per job.
