TAKE A NUMBER

Your number is real. So is the one being served. They will never meet.

Take a ticket from the machine under the NOW SERVING board and hold it through eight lines. The market aisle slides with you until you walk backwards. The bouncer lets the line move only while you are not looking at him. The deli cutter takes your order and the man by the pickles takes your ticket, and losing it is fifty dollars. The drop moves only when you stand still and sells out at 11:01. Santaland's exit sends you back to the entrance twice. The bathroom door is occupied while you look at it. Then stand on the gold star under the Grand Central clock and your number comes up. Finally.
