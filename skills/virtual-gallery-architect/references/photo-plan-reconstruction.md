# Faithful reconstruction from photographs and plans

Use this mode when the user wants an existing place reconstructed accurately. User corrections, original plans, and actual photographs outrank previous models and renders. Do not introduce a brand remix, widened gallery circulation, or an invented palette unless requested.

## Establish evidence before geometry

Inventory sources and retain originals. Record which file supports each room, material, opening, and furnishing orientation. Look for original plan screenshots among nearby project files when a digitized plan has no provenance. An old model is reusable geometry, not independent evidence.

Trace the footprint, stepped service cores, corridor jogs, and openings before fitting furniture. Calibrate plan axes and separately drawn floor insets independently. Check room overlap and adjacency. Label inferred dimensions and unknown rooms; a traced screenshot is not a measured survey.

Use paired photo views to establish orientation: window wall, opposing cabinets, island direction, and room-to-room sightlines. Scaling old furniture into a new room does not establish its accuracy.

## Materials and openings

Doorways, pass-throughs, and stair openings must cut the actual wall or slab. Validate the view through them after furniture is restored; an opening can still be blocked by a second wall or cabinet.

Inspect rendered colors beside the source photos. Keep one deliberate linear-to-display conversion through postprocessing. Averaging a procedural shader's color ramp is not texture baking. Separate photo textures, procedural materials, and approximate furnishings in the provenance notes.

A mesh-sampled Gaussian surfel layer is not a trained Gaussian scan. State the distinction and keep structural meshes authoritative for navigation and openings.

## Artwork and verification

Use identified artwork images with provenance, preserved aspect ratio, and neutral display material. Do not label invented procedural paintings as works by a named artist. Check mounts against doors, bookcases, and room sightlines. Distinguish the user's requested curation from the apartment's historic collection.

Validate representative rooms on the actual GPU, including an opening, dark wood, saturated upholstery, artwork, and the whole plan. Verify the browser loaded the new asset revision before judging a change. Record remaining uncertainty rather than claiming photographic or survey accuracy.
