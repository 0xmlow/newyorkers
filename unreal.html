DEPARTMENT OF UNREAL ESTATE

Reality is rent stabilized

A loft that cost more than it looks. The bagel on the plinth includes your security deposit. The studio at the back is seven metres deep and reads as forty. Stand on the yellow ring and the pieces in the air close into one circle; take a step and it breaks. Sit on the bench at the back and watch the commute move while you do not. Four sets of twelve New Yorkers hang in the bays: the listing, the move, the building and the unreal.
