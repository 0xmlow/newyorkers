N3W YORKERS SPACE PROGRAM works on a phone: left thumb walks, right thumb looks, tap anything.
WASD walk, SHIFT hurry, mouse to look, click anything, M for sound, N or WAY OUT for a line on the floor to the next door, [ and ] for the previous and next room, R for a random room, K to skate. Space pushes off in zero G, jump on the moon is a sixth of yours.

N3W YORKERS SPACE PROGRAM

Funded by the fare hike. T minus ten.

The countdown only runs while nobody is looking at it, so look away. Walk up to the rocket on Rockaway Beach and the beach gets wider. Float through a retired R46 car with the bagels and the fare card. Leave footprints on the Sheep Meadow of the moon and move the lander before the sweeper comes. Order a coffee on Mars and wait three seconds for the answer. Point the Hubble at the courtyard and find the window doing something wrong. Whisper into a dish and Astoria answers. Then come down next to the ferry, which did not stop.
